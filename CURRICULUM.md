# Lojban 30-Week Curriculum

**15 minutes per day, 7 days a week, no rest days.**
Detailed daily lessons are in `week-01.md` through `week-30.md`.
Course design guidelines are in `PROJECT.md`.

---

## Design Principles

- **Phonology & numbers are easy** — covered in 3 weeks, not belabored.
- **`la` is the only article** — no article zoo.
- **Place structure is the core challenge** — 6 weeks dedicated (Weeks 4-9), with drills returning throughout.
- **`se/te/ve/xe` is mind-bending** — introduced one operator per week, with dedicated practice.
- **Tanru and lujvo need heavy repetition** — 5 weeks dedicated (Weeks 10-14).
- **Spaced repetition built in** — every week refreshes prior vocab and grammar.

## Phase 1: First Contact (Weeks 1-3)

Get comfortable with Lojban sounds, make your first sentences, learn the basics.

| Week | Title | New Grammar | New Vocab | Total Vocab |
|------|-------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | Sounds, Words & Your First Bridi | Phonology, word types (gismu/cmavo/cmevla), bridi = selbri + sumti, `cu`, `.i`, pronouns (mi/do/ti/ta/tu) | 12 gismu | 12 |
| 2 | `la`, Names & Greetings | `la` (the one article), Lojbanizing names, vocatives (coi/co'o/ki'e/je'e/mi'e) | 8 gismu | 20 |
| 3 | Numbers & Yes/No Questions | Digits (no-so), compound numbers, `xu` (yes/no question), `go'i`/`na go'i` | 8 gismu | 28 |

**Milestone:** Can pronounce Lojban, greet people, count, ask yes/no questions.

---

## Phase 2: Place Structure — The Core Challenge (Weeks 4-9)

The hardest and most important part of Lojban. Every gismu has a fixed argument structure (x1 x2 x3...). This phase teaches you to fill, swap, and tag those places.

| Week | Title | New Grammar | New Vocab | Total Vocab |
|------|-------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| 4 | What Place Structure Means | Place structure (x1 x2 x3...), filling left-to-right, `zo'e` (unspecified) | 10 gismu | 38 |
| 5 | Multi-place Predicate Drills | 2/3/4/5-place gismu drills, common mistakes | 10 gismu | 48 |
| 6 | `se` — First Conversion | `se` swaps x1↔x2, foregrounding, passive-like constructions | 8 gismu | 56 |
| 7 | `te`, `ve`, `xe` — Full Conversion | `te` (x1↔x3), `ve` (x1↔x4), `xe` (x1↔x5), stacking | 8 gismu | 64 |
| 8 | `fa fe fi fo fu` Place Tags | Explicit place labeling, mixing with defaults, tags vs conversion | 8 gismu | 72 |
| 9 | Place Structure Review | Comprehensive drills: conversion ↔ tags, error correction, complex scenarios | 8 gismu | 80 |

**Milestone:** Can fill place structures correctly, use `se/te/ve/xe` conversion and `fa/fe/fi/fo/fu` tags.

---

## Phase 3: Tanru & Compound Words (Weeks 10-14)

Build infinite concepts from finite vocabulary. Tanru (loose compounds) and lujvo (fixed compounds) are Lojban's word-building engine.

| Week | Title | New Grammar | New Vocab | Total Vocab |
|------|-------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| 10 | Two-word Tanru | Left modifies right, place structure from rightmost word, vagueness | 10 gismu | 90 |
| 11 | Tanru Grouping | Default left-to-right, `bo` (tight binding), `ke...ke'e` (explicit brackets) | 8 gismu | 98 |
| 12 | Rafsi & Lujvo | Rafsi (combining forms), lujvo construction, `y` buffer, jbovlaste | 8 lujvo | 106 |
| 13 | `co` & Fu'ivla | `co` (tanru inversion), fu'ivla (borrowed words), morphology rules | 8 gismu | 114 |
| 14 | Compound Words Review | Full review: build tanru → freeze to lujvo, decompose lujvo, create fu'ivla | 8 gismu | 122 |

**Milestone:** Can build tanru, decompose lujvo, create compound words.

---

## Phase 4: Sentence Expansion (Weeks 15-20)

Add time, negation, emotions, embedded clauses, and other expressive tools to your sentences.

| Week | Title | New Grammar | New Vocab | Total Vocab |
|------|-------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| 15 | Tense: Time & Aspect | Tense is optional; `pu/ca/ba`, contours (`co'a/ca'o/mo'u/co'u`), duration (`ze'i/ze'a/ze'u`) | 10 gismu | 132 |
| 16 | Negation | `na` (bridi), `na'e` (scalar), `to'e` (opposite), `no'e` (neutral) | 8 gismu | 140 |
| 17 | Questions Deep Dive | `ma` (sumti question), `mo` (selbri question), `xo` (number question), multiple `ma` | 8 gismu | 148 |
| 18 | Attitudinals & Evidentials | `.ui/.oi/.a'o/.au/...`, intensity scale (`cai/sai/ru'e/cu'i/nai`), evidentials (`ba'a/ti'e/za'a/pe'i`) | 15 cmavo | 148 + 15 cmavo |
| 19 | Abstractions | `nu` (event), `du'u` (proposition), `ka` (property), `ni` (amount), `kei` terminator | 8 gismu | 156 |
| 20 | Relative Clauses | `poi...ku'o` (restrictive), `noi...ku'o` (incidental), `ke'a`, `pe`/`ne` | 8 gismu | 164 |

**Milestone:** Can use tense, negation, attitudinals, abstractions, and relative clauses.

## Phase 5: Connecting & Expanding (Weeks 21-26)

Connect ideas, add context, quote speech, and build complex nested structures.

| Week | Title | New Grammar | New Vocab | Total Vocab |
|------|-------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| 21 | Logical Connectives | Sumti (`.e/.a/.o/.u`), selbri (`je/ja/jo/ju`), bridi-tail (`gi'e/gi'a`), forethought (`ge...gi/ga...gi`) | 8 gismu | 172 |
| 22 | Sumtcita (Modal Tags) | `ri'a/mu'i/ki'u` (cause), `bu'u/fa'a/to'o` (location), `pi'o/ta'i` (method), `bau` (language) | 8 gismu | 180 |
| 23 | `be`/`bei`/`be'o` | Binding places inside descriptions: `la vecnu be la .skami.` (seller of computers) | 8 gismu | 188 |
| 24 | Quotation & Discursives | `lu...li'u`, `zo`, `lo'u...le'u`, `la'e`/`lu'e`; discursives: `ku'i/ji'a/po'o/mu'a` | 8 gismu | 196 |
| 25 | Advanced Tense & Space | Spatial tense (`vi/va/vu`), compound tenses, `ki` (sticky), `roi` (repetition) | 8 gismu | 204 |
| 26 | Complex Sentence Nesting | Nested `nu`/`du'u`, `tu'a` (raising), conversion inside abstractions, parsing long bridi | 8 gismu | 212 |

**Milestone:** Can use connectives, modals, quotation, and complex nested structures.

---

## Phase 6: Fluency & Practice (Weeks 27-30)

Apply everything. Converse, read, write, and polish the edges.

| Week | Title | Focus | New Vocab | Total Vocab |
|------|-------|-------|-----------|-------------|
| 27 | Everyday Conversations | Introductions, family, work, food, travel, opinions — no new grammar | 8 gismu | 220 |
| 28 | Reading, Writing & Storytelling | Parse texts, write journal entries, narrate stories, reported speech | 8 gismu | 228 |
| 29 | Advanced Topics Sampler | MEX (math), pro-bridi (`broda`), terminator elision, non-logical connectives (`joi/ce/ce'o`) | 8 gismu | 236 |
| 30 | Final Review & Graduation | Comprehensive review marathon, free composition, self-assessment, next steps | 0 | 236 |

**Milestone:** Can read, write, and converse in basic Lojban. ~236 vocabulary items. Ready for immersion.

## Difficulty Map

```
Easy (learn fast, move on)        Hard (spend weeks here, keep drilling)
──────────────────────────        ─────────────────────────────────────
Phonology (6 vowels, easy cons)   Place structure (x1 x2 x3 x4 x5)
Numbers (just string digits)      se/te/ve/xe conversion
la (one article, that's it)       fa/fe/fi/fo/fu (tags are escape hatch)
Greetings (coi, co'o, ki'e)      Tanru grouping (bo, ke...ke'e, co)
Attitudinals (fun, learn by use)  Lujvo building (rafsi + morphology)
                                  Abstractions (nu, du'u, ka — bridi in bridi)
                                  Nested structures (stacking all of the above)
```

## Weekly File Format

Each `week-NN.md` contains:

1. **Vocabulary to Learn** — new gismu/cmavo/lujvo with definitions and place structures
2. **Grammar to Learn** — new concepts with explanations and examples
3. **Vocabulary to Refresh** — cumulative words from prior weeks
4. **Grammar to Refresh** — prior concepts that connect to this week's topic
5. **Conversation Topics** — practical things to try saying
6. **Day 1-7 Breakdown** — specific 15-minute activities for each day
