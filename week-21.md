# Week 21 — Logical Connectives

## 1. Vocabulary to Learn

- **jmina** — x1 adds/increases x2 to/by amount x3
- **vimcu** — x1 removes/subtracts/takes away x2 from x3
- **pagbu** — x1 is a part/component of x2
- **stura** — x1 is a structure of x2
- **ciste** — x1 is a system with components x2 and structure x3
- **frica** — x1 differs/is different from x2 in property x3
- **simsa** — x1 is similar to x2 in property x3
- **mintu** — x1 is the same as x2 in property x3

## 2. Grammar to Learn

**Logical Connectives** — Joining sumti, selbri, or bridi with logical relationships:

**Sumti Connectives (join arguments):**
- **.e** — and (both): `mi .e do` (me and you)
- **.a** — or (at least one): `mi .a do` (me or you or both)
- **.o** — iff (same truth value): `mi .o do` (me if and only if you)
- **.u** — whether or not (independent): `mi .u do` (me whether or not you)

**Selbri Connectives (join predicates):**
- **je** — and: `melbi je xamgu` (beautiful and good)
- **ja** — or: `melbi ja xamgu` (beautiful or good)
- **jo** — iff: `melbi jo xamgu` (beautiful iff good)
- **ju** — whether or not: `melbi ju xamgu` (beautiful whether or not good)

**Bridi-tail Connectives (join predicates with different arguments):**
- **gi'e** — and: `mi klama gi'e cadzu` (I go and walk)
- **gi'a** — or: `mi klama gi'a cadzu` (I go or walk)
- **gi'o** — iff: `mi klama gi'o cadzu` (I go iff I walk)
- **gi'u** — whether or not: `mi klama gi'u cadzu` (I go whether or not I walk)

**Forethought (connective comes first):**
- **ge ... gi** — both ... and: `ge mi gi do cu klama` (both I and you go)
- **ga ... gi** — either ... or: `ga mi gi do cu klama` (either I or you go)
- **go ... gi** — iff: `go mi gi do cu klama` (I go iff you go)
- **gu ... gi** — whether or not

**Negation with Connectives:**
- **na.e** — and-not (A but not B)
- **.enai** — not-and (B but not A)
- **na.enai** — nor (neither A nor B)
- **naja** — only if (A only if B)

## 3. Vocabulary to Refresh

Focus on combining concepts:
- **klama** — go
- **cadzu** — walk
- **tavla** — talk
- **melbi** — beautiful
- **xamgu** — good
- **xlali** — bad
- **barda** — big
- **cmalu** — small

## 4. Grammar to Refresh

- **Bridi structure** — Understanding what you're connecting
- **Negation (na)** — How it interacts with connectives
- **Selbri** — Recognizing when to use je vs gi'e
- **Place structure** — Shared vs different places

## 5. Conversation Topics

Practice saying:
- "I and you go to Paris."
- "The dog is big and beautiful."
- "I go or walk (or both)."
- "Both Alice and Bob are happy."
- "Either I go or you go."
- "I walk and sing."
- "Neither I nor you understand."

## Day 1 — Sumti Connectives: .e

**15 min:** Learn to connect sumti with .e (and).

- Read grammar section on sumti connectives
- Memorize: jmina, vimcu
- Practice: `mi .e do cu klama` (I and you go)
- Build 3 sentences with .e connecting different sumti
- Note: both sumti share the same relationship to the selbri

## Day 2 — Other Sumti Connectives

**15 min:** Learn .a (or), .o (iff), .u (whether or not).

- Read grammar on .a, .o, .u
- Memorize: pagbu, stura
- Practice: `mi .a do cu klama` (I or you go)
- Distinguish: .e (both), .a (at least one), .o (same), .u (independent)
- Build 3 sentences using .a

## Day 3 — Selbri Connectives: je/ja

**15 min:** Learn to connect predicates with je (and), ja (or).

- Read grammar section on selbri connectives
- Memorize: ciste, frica
- Practice: `la gerku cu barda je melbi` (the dog is big and beautiful)
- Contrast: sumti connectives vs selbri connectives
- Build 3 sentences with je, 3 with ja

## Day 4 — Bridi-tail Connectives: gi'e

**15 min:** Learn gi'e to connect predicates with different arguments.

- Read grammar section on bridi-tail connectives
- Memorize: simsa, mintu
- Practice: `mi klama la .paris. gi'e cadzu la .london.` (I go to Paris and walk in London)
- When to use gi'e vs je (different vs shared places)
- Build 3 sentences with gi'e

## Day 5 — Forethought Connectives

**15 min:** Learn ge...gi (both...and), ga...gi (either...or).

- Read grammar section on forethought
- Review all 8 vocabulary words
- Practice: `ge mi gi do cu klama` (both I and you go)
- Why forethought? Clarity in complex sentences
- Build 2 sentences with ge...gi, 2 with ga...gi

## Day 6 — Negation with Connectives

**15 min:** Learn na.e, .enai, na.enai.

- Read grammar section on negation with connectives
- Practice: `mi na.e do cu klama` (I but not you go)
- Contrast: na.e (A not B), .enai (B not A), na.enai (neither)
- Build 3 sentences using negated connectives

## Day 7 — Review and Integration

**15 min:** Comprehensive review of week 21 material.

- Quiz yourself on all 8 vocabulary words
- Write 2 sentences each with: .e, je, gi'e, ge...gi
- Practice distinguishing sumti vs selbri vs bridi-tail connectives
- Create complex sentences: "I and you are both tall and beautiful."
