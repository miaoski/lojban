# Week 16 — Negation

## 1. Vocabulary to Learn

**8 new gismu:**
- **birti** — x1 is certain/sure that x2 is true
- **senpi** — x1 doubts/is skeptical of x2
- **jetnu** — x1 (proposition) is true
- **jitfa** — x1 (proposition) is false
- **xusra** — x1 asserts/claims x2 (proposition)
- **tugni** — x1 agrees with x2 about x3 (proposition)
- **natfe** — x1 denies/refutes x2 (proposition)
- **drani** — x1 is correct/right about x2 by standard x3

## 2. Grammar to Learn

### `na` — Bridi Negation
- **Negates the entire bridi:** `mi na klama` (I don't go / It's not true that I go)
- **Placement:** Goes before the selbri, after `cu`
- **With tense:** `mi na pu klama` (It's not true that I went) vs. `mi pu na klama` (I went-not = I didn't go)
- **Scope:** Negates the whole claim, not just one place

### `na'e` — Scalar Negation (Other-Than)
- **Means "other than":** `mi na'e klama` (I other-than-go / I do something other than going)
- **Unspecified alternative:** You're doing something else, but what? Not specified
- **Use case:** When you want to deny something without being specific about the alternative

### `to'e` — Polar Opposite
- **Means "opposite of":** `to'e melbi` (ugly / opposite of beautiful)
- **Creates antonyms:** `to'e xamgu` (bad), `to'e barda` (tiny)
- **Strong negation:** Maximum opposite on a scale

### `no'e` — Neutral Midpoint
- **Means "neutral/neither":** `no'e melbi` (neither beautiful nor ugly)
- **Middle of the scale:** Not at either extreme
- **Use case:** Expressing neutrality

### Negation Scale Summary
- **to'e** (polar opposite) — **na'e** (other than) — **no'e** (neutral) — [unnegated] (affirmed)

## 3. Vocabulary to Refresh

**All prior weeks (~130 words)** — Focus on:
- Opposites: melbi/to'e melbi, xamgu/xlali, barda/cmalu
- Actions to negate: klama, citka, djica, nelci
- State predicates: birti, senpi

## 4. Grammar to Refresh

- **Bridi structure:** Understanding what `na` negates
- **Tense interaction:** `na` + tense placement matters
  - `mi na pu klama` — not true that [I went]
  - `mi pu na klama` — [I past] don't-go = I didn't go
- **Place structure:** Negation applies to the whole bridi, not individual places

## 5. Conversation Topics

**Topic:** Disagree with statements and express denial

**Practice scenarios:**
1. Correct false statements: "That's not true"
2. Express what you don't want: "I don't want that"
3. Describe opposites: "It's the opposite of good"
4. Express uncertainty: "I'm not certain about that"

**Example sentences:**
- `mi na djica lo plise` — I don't want the apple
- `ti na jetnu` — This is not true
- `ti to'e xamgu` — This is the opposite of good (this is bad)
- `mi na'e nelci ti` — I other-than-like this (I feel something other than liking)
- `mi na birti lo du'u do klama` — I'm not certain that you go
- `mi natfe lo du'u ti xamgu` — I deny that this is good

## Day 1 — Truth Vocabulary (15 min)

- Learn birti, senpi, jetnu, jitfa
- Practice: `mi birti lo du'u ti jetnu` (I'm certain that this is true)
- Practice: `mi senpi lo du'u ti jetnu` (I doubt that this is true)
- Drill: Make 5 sentences about certainty and truth

## Day 2 — Agreement Vocabulary (15 min)

- Learn xusra, tugni, natfe, drani
- Practice: `mi xusra lo du'u ti xamgu` (I assert that this is good)
- Practice: `mi tugni do` (I agree with you)
- Practice: `mi natfe ti` (I deny this)
- Drill: Express agreement and denial in 5 scenarios

## Day 3 — `na` Bridi Negation (15 min)

- Learn `na` negates the entire bridi
- Practice: `mi na klama` (I don't go)
- Practice: `ti na xamgu` (This is not good)
- Practice: `mi na djica lo plise` (I don't want apples)
- Drill: Negate 10 positive sentences

## Day 4 — `na` with Tense (15 min)

- Learn placement affects meaning
- Practice: `mi na pu klama` (not-true that [I went])
- Practice: `mi pu na klama` (I past-not-go = I didn't go)
- Compare: Different scopes of negation
- Drill: Create 5 pairs showing the difference

## Day 5 — Scalar Negation (`na'e`, `to'e`, `no'e`) (15 min)

- Learn `na'e` (other-than), `to'e` (opposite), `no'e` (neutral)
- Practice: `ti to'e melbi` (this is ugly)
- Practice: `ti no'e melbi` (this is neither beautiful nor ugly)
- Practice: `mi na'e klama` (I other-than-go)
- Drill: Create negation scales for 5 properties

## Day 6 — Negation Practice (15 min)

- Combine all negation types
- Practice denial: `mi natfe lo du'u ti to'e xamgu` (I deny that this is the opposite of good)
- Practice disagreement: `mi na tugni` (I don't agree)
- Express uncertainty: `mi na birti` (I'm not certain)
- Drill: 10 sentences mixing negation types

## Day 7 — Conversation & Review (15 min)

- Practice disagreeing politely in conversation
- Correct 5 false statements using appropriate negation
- Express preferences using negation: "I don't want X, I want Y"
- Self-test: Translate 10 negative sentences
- Review: When to use na vs. na'e vs. to'e
- Final practice: Debate a topic using all negation tools
