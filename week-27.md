# Week 27 — Everyday Conversations

## Vocabulary to Learn

1. **pendo** — x1 is a friend of x2 (mutual relationship)
2. **bruna** — x1 is a brother of x2
3. **mensi** — x1 is a sister of x2
4. **mamta** — x1 is a mother of x2
5. **patfu** — x1 is a father of x2
6. **panzi** — x1 is an offspring/child of x2
7. **jatna** — x1 is a captain/leader of x2 in activity x3
8. **kansa** — x1 accompanies/is with x2 in doing x3

## Grammar to Learn

**PRACTICE WEEK** — No major new grammar. Focus on fluent application of all tools learned in weeks 1-26.

**Conversational Structures:**

1. **Introductions:** `mi'e [name]` (I'm...), `do cmene ma` (What's your name?)
2. **Family talk:** Use kinship terms with place structure awareness
3. **Asking about work/hobbies:** `do zukte ma` (What do you do?), `do nelci ma` (What do you like?)
4. **Expressing feelings:** Combine attitudinals with abstractions — `.ui mi gleki lo nu penmi do`
5. **Ordering/shopping:** `mi djica lo nu te vecnu [item]` (I want to buy...)
6. **Directions:** Use spatial tenses and gismu from week 25
7. **Opinions in dialogue:** `xu do jinvi lo du'u...` (Do you think that...?)

**Goal:** String together 10+ exchange dialogues using natural Lojban flow.

## Vocabulary to Refresh

All approximately 202 gismu learned in weeks 1-26. Focus on using them in conversational contexts rather than isolated sentences.

## Grammar to Refresh

**Everything, with focus on natural combination:**
- Place structure + conversion (switching perspectives in conversation)
- Tanru (describing people, objects naturally)
- Abstraction (expressing wants, beliefs, knowledge)
- Connectives (building multi-clause responses)
- Attitudinals (adding emotion and attitude to dialogue)
- Tense (narrating, describing when things happened)
- Questions (xu, ma, mo)

## Conversation Topics

**Full realistic dialogues:**
1. Meeting someone new: greetings → introduce yourself → ask about them → find common interests → goodbye
2. Family discussion: talk about your family members, their occupations, relationships
3. Planning an outing: suggest activity → agree on details → arrange time/place
4. At a restaurant: greet server → order food → request changes → express satisfaction
5. Giving/receiving directions: ask where something is → explain route using spatial terms
6. Discussing studies: what you're learning → why it's important → difficulties and progress
7. Sharing opinions: express view → support with reasoning → acknowledge other perspectives

Aim for at least 10 back-and-forth exchanges per dialogue.

## Day 1 — Introductions and Greetings

**15 minutes:**
- Learn pendo, bruna, mensi (5 min)
- Practice introduction dialogue: `coi .i mi'e [name] .i xu do se pendo mi` (5 min)
- Role-play meeting a new person, exchanging names and basic info (5 min)

## Day 2 — Family Talk

**15 minutes:**
- Learn mamta, patfu, panzi (5 min)
- Describe your family structure: `la [name] cu mamta mi` (3 min)
- Practice Q&A about family: `do se bruna ma` (How many brothers do you have?) (7 min)

## Day 3 — Work and Activities

**15 minutes:**
- Learn jatna, kansa (3 min)
- Practice asking/answering about jobs and hobbies (7 min)
- Use abstractions: `mi nelci lo nu [activity]` (5 min)

## Day 4 — Shopping and Ordering

**15 minutes:**
- Build restaurant/shop dialogue (10 min)
- Use: `mi djica lo nu te vecnu...` / `mi djica tu'a...` (3 min)
- Practice polite requests and responses with attitudinals (2 min)

## Day 5 — Directions and Locations

**15 minutes:**
- Combine week 25 spatial vocab with conversational frame (7 min)
- Practice: "Where is X?" → "It's to the left of Y, inside Z" (5 min)
- Give directions to navigate a building or area (3 min)

## Day 6 — Opinions and Discussion

**15 minutes:**
- Practice expressing and asking for opinions (7 min)
- Use nested abstractions naturally: `xu do jinvi lo du'u...` (5 min)
- Build a mini-debate: state view → give reason → respond to counterpoint (3 min)

## Day 7 — Free Conversation Practice

**15 minutes:**
- Full 10+ exchange dialogue on any topic (12 min)
- Combine all elements: emotion, tense, complex sentences, questions (3 min)
- Self-assess fluency and naturalness
