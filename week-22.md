# Week 22 — Sumtcita (Modal Tags)

## 1. Vocabulary to Learn

- **dargu** — x1 is a road/highway to x2 from x3
- **foldi** — x1 is a field of x2 (crop/property)
- **cmana** — x1 is a mountain/hill in terrain x2
- **rirxe** — x1 is a river flowing to x2 from x3
- **xamsi** — x1 is a sea/ocean of fluid x2
- **kevna** — x1 is a cave/cavern in x2
- **stuzi** — x1 is a place/location at x2
- **tcana** — x1 is a station/stop on route x2

## 2. Grammar to Learn

**Sumtcita (Modal Tags)** — Adding contextual information beyond basic place structure:

Like precise prepositions that extend the bridi with additional context.

**Cause/Reason:**
- **ri'a** — physical cause: `mi farlu ri'a lo nu mi xagji` (I fall because-physically I'm hungry)
- **mu'i** — motivation: `mi klama mu'i lo nu mi djica` (I go motivated-by my desire)
- **ki'u** — justification/explanation: `mi klama ki'u lo nu xamgu` (I go justified-by it being good)
- **ni'i** — logical reason: `la .alis. cu ninmu ni'i lo nu ri se cmene zo .alis.` (Alice is female logically-because she is named Alice)

**Location:**
- **bu'u** — at (location): `mi cadzu bu'u la .paris.` (I walk at Paris)
- **fa'a** — toward/facing: `mi klama fa'a la .cmana.` (I go toward the mountain)
- **to'o** — away from: `mi bajra to'o la .gerku.` (I run away-from the dog)
- **ne'a** — near: `mi cpana ne'a la .rirxe.` (I am near the river)
- **ru'u** — surrounding: `lo dargu cu sruri ru'u lo dinju` (the road surrounds the house)

**Instrument/Method:**
- **pi'o** — using (tool/agent): `mi ciska pi'o lo penbi` (I write using the pen)
- **ta'i** — by method: `mi snada ta'i lo nu troci` (I succeed by-method-of trying)
- **se pi'o** — used by: `lo penbi cu se pi'o mi ciska` (the pen is used-by me to-write)

**Language:**
- **bau** — in language: `mi tavla bau la .jbobau.` (I speak in Lojban)

**Result:**
- **ja'e** — with result: `mi citka ja'e lo nu mi tolxagji` (I eat with-result I'm not hungry)
- **se ja'e** — as a result of: `mi tolxagji se ja'e lo nu mi citka` (I'm not hungry as-a-result-of eating)

**Time:**
- **ca** — at time (also basic tense)
- **de'i** — on date: `mi jbena de'i li 1990` (I was born on 1990)

**Terminator:**
- **cu** separates sumtcita from selbri
- **ku** can terminate the sumti after a sumtcita if needed

## 3. Vocabulary to Refresh

Focus on place vocabulary and verbs:
- **klama** — go (to where? sumtcita adds detail)
- **cadzu** — walk
- **bajra** — run
- **ciska** — write
- **tavla** — talk
- **citka** — eat
- **farlu** — fall
- **snada** — succeed
- **troci** — try

## 4. Grammar to Refresh

- **Place structure** — Sumtcita extend beyond the 5 places
- **fa/fe/fi/fo/fu** — Place tags vs modal tags
- **Tenses (pu/ca/ba)** — Some sumtcita overlap with tenses
- **cu separator** — Essential with sumtcita

## 5. Conversation Topics

Practice saying:
- "I walk to the mountain."
- "I write using a pen."
- "I speak in Lojban because I like it."
- "I run away from the dog."
- "I succeed by trying hard."
- "I was born on [date]."
- "I eat, and as a result, I'm not hungry."
