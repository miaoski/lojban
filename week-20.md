# Week 20 — Relative Clauses: poi, noi, pe

## 1. Vocabulary to Learn

- **lisri** — x1 is a story/tale/narrative about x2
- **nuzba** — x1 is news/tidings about x2 from source x3
- **pixra** — x1 is a picture/image of x2 made by x3
- **sanga** — x1 sings/chants x2 (song/melody)
- **zgike** — x1 is music performed by x2
- **pemci** — x1 is a poem/verse about x2 by poet x3
- **draci** — x1 is a drama/play about x2 by dramatist x3
- **finti** — x1 invents/creates/composes x2 for purpose x3

## 2. Grammar to Learn

**Relative Clauses** — Modifying sumti with additional bridi:

- **poi ... ku'o** — restrictive relative clause (identifies WHICH one)
  - `la prenu poi tavla mi ku'o cu klama` (the person who talks to me goes)
  - Essential to identify the referent
  - Narrows down which thing you're talking about

- **noi ... ku'o** — incidental/non-restrictive relative clause (adds extra info)
  - `la .alis. noi ninmu ku'o cu klama` (Alice, who is a woman, goes)
  - Provides additional information
  - The referent is already identified

- **ke'a** — back-reference pronoun (refers to the modified sumti inside the clause)
  - `la prenu poi mi prami ke'a cu melbi` (the person whom I love is beautiful)
  - Default: ke'a is x1 of the relative clause bridi

- **pe** — short restrictive association
  - `la zdani pe mi` (the house of-me / my house)
  - Quick way to show association/possession

- **ne** — short incidental association
  - `la cukta ne la .alis.` (the book, incidentally Alice's)

- **ku'o** — relative clause terminator (often elidable at end of bridi)

## 3. Vocabulary to Refresh

Focus on verbs useful in relative clauses:
- **ponse** — own/possess
- **prami** — love
- **nelci** — like
- **tavla** — talk
- **tcidu** — read
- **ciska** — write
- **finti** — create
- **vecnu** — sell
- **lebna** — take
- **dunda** — give

## 4. Grammar to Refresh

- **la** — The article for names (essential in relative clauses)
- **Tanru** — Relative clauses are like tanru but more explicit
- **Pronouns** — mi/do work inside relative clauses
- **Place structure** — Understanding places helps with ke'a usage

## 5. Conversation Topics

Practice describing:
- "The person who sings is my friend."
- "Alice, who is tall, goes to the store."
- "The book that I read is interesting."
- "My dog is beautiful."
- "The story that you told is funny."
- "The person whom I love is kind."

## Day 1 — Introduction to poi

**15 min:** Learn restrictive relative clauses with poi.

- Read grammar section on poi
- Memorize: lisri, nuzba
- Practice: `la gerku poi mi ponse cu melbi` (the dog that I own is beautiful)
- Build 3 sentences using poi to identify which thing you mean

## Day 2 — The ke'a Pronoun

**15 min:** Learn to use ke'a as back-reference.

- Read grammar section on ke'a
- Memorize: pixra, sanga
- Practice: `la prenu poi mi prami ke'a cu klama` (the person whom I love goes)
- When is ke'a needed? When it's not x1 of the relative clause
- Build 3 sentences with ke'a in non-x1 positions

## Day 3 — Incidental noi Clauses

**15 min:** Learn non-restrictive clauses with noi.

- Read grammar section on noi
- Memorize: zgike, pemci
- Practice: `la .alis. noi ninmu cu klama` (Alice, who is a woman, goes)
- Contrast poi (which one?) vs noi (by the way...)
- Build 3 sentences with noi adding extra information

## Day 4 — Short Forms: pe and ne

**15 min:** Learn abbreviated relative clauses.

- Read grammar section on pe and ne
- Memorize: draci, finti
- Practice: `la zdani pe mi` (my house), `la cukta ne la .alis.` (the book, Alice's)
- When to use short forms vs full clauses
- Build 3 sentences with pe and 3 with ne

## Day 5 — Complex Relative Clauses

**15 min:** Build more complex sentences with nested structures.

- Review all 8 vocabulary words
- Practice: `la prenu poi finti lo pemci ku'o cu sanga` (the person who creates poems sings)
- Combine relative clauses with abstractions from week 19
- Build 3 complex sentences

## Day 6 — Conversation Practice

**15 min:** Describe people and things using relative clauses.

- Practice: "The singer whom I heard is famous." — `la sanga poi mi tirna ke'a cu cmalu` (adjust for "famous")
- "My friend, who is tall, reads the book that I wrote."
- Create dialogues describing people with poi/noi
- Build 5 conversational exchanges

## Day 7 — Review and Integration

**15 min:** Comprehensive review of week 20 material.

- Quiz yourself on all 8 vocabulary words
- Write 5 sentences: 2 with poi, 2 with noi, 1 with pe/ne
- Practice distinguishing when to use restrictive vs incidental
- Review ku'o terminator usage
