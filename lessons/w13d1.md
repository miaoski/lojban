# Week 13 · Day 1 — Temperature and Moisture Vocabulary

> **Today's goal:** Learn words for temperature and moisture.
> **Time:** 15 minutes

Quick tanru review:

---

## New Material (5-7 min)

### New Vocabulary

| Lojban | Place Structure | English |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| `glare` | x1 is hot/warm at temperature x2 | hot/warm |
| `lenku` | x1 is cold/cool at temperature x2 | cold/cool |
| `cilmo` | x1 is moist/wet with liquid x2 | moist/wet |
| `sudga` | x1 is dry of liquid x2 | dry |

### Temperature Words

```
lo djacu cu glare
```
The water is hot.

```
lo zdani cu lenku
```
The house is cold.

### Moisture Words

```
lo foldi cu cilmo
```
The field is wet.

```
lo cukta cu sudga
```
The book is dry.

### In Tanru

```
ti glare djacu
```
This is hot water.

```
ta lenku zdani
```
That is a cold house.

### Key Point

> These four words describe physical properties — temperature and moisture — common in daily descriptions.

## Practice (4-5 min)

### Exercise 1: Temperature Sentences

Translate:

1. The food is hot.
2. The room is cold.
3. I am warm.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. `lo cidja cu glare`
2. `lo kumfa cu lenku`
3. `mi glare`

</details>

### Exercise 2: Moisture Sentences

Translate:

1. The ground is wet.
2. The towel is dry.
3. My clothes are moist.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. `lo dertu cu cilmo`
2. `lo tivni cu sudga` (or use appropriate word for towel)
3. `lo mi taxfu cu cilmo`

</details>

### Exercise 3: Combine in Tanru

Create tanru:

1. cold water → ?
2. hot food → ?
3. wet ground → ?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. `lenku djacu`
2. `glare cidja`
3. `cilmo dertu`

</details>
