## New Material (5-7 min)

### New Vocabulary

| Lojban | Place Structure | English |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| `gasnu` | x1 (agent) causes/brings about x2 | make happen |
| `zukte` | x1 does/acts to bring about x2 | do/act |
| `troci` | x1 tries to do x2 | try |
| `snada` | x1 succeeds in doing x2 | succeed |

### Doing and Acting

```
mi zukte ti
```
I do this.

```
do zukte ma
```
What do you do? (What action do you take?)

### Causing as Agent

```
mi gasnu lo nu ti slabu
```
I bring about this being old. (I make this old.)

Note: `gasnu` = agent causes, `rinka` = event causes.

### Trying and Succeeding

```
mi troci lo nu klama
```
I try to go.

```
mi snada lo nu gunka
```
I succeed in working.

### Key Point

> `zukte` = do/act, `gasnu` = make happen, `troci` = try, `snada` = succeed.

## Practice (4-5 min)

### Exercise 1: Doing

Translate:

1. I do this.
2. What do you do?
3. Bob does that.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. `mi zukte ti`
2. `do zukte ma`
3. `la .bob. cu zukte ta`

</details>

### Exercise 2: Trying

Translate:

1. I try to go.
2. She tries to work.
3. We try to understand.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. `mi troci lo nu klama`
2. `ri troci lo nu gunka`
3. `mi'o troci lo nu jimpe`

</details>

### Exercise 3: Succeeding

Translate:

1. I succeed.
2. Bob succeeds in learning.
3. Do you succeed?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. `mi snada`
2. `la .bob. cu snada lo nu cilre`
3. `xu do snada`

</details>
