## New Material (5-7 min)

### New Vocabulary

| Lojban | Place Structure | English |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| `cerni` | x1 is morning of day x2 at location x3 | morning |
| `vanci` | x1 is evening of day x2 at location x3 | evening |
| `nicte` | x1 is night of day x2 at location x3 | night |
| `temci` | x1 is a time/duration | time |
| `detri` | x1 is the date of event x2 at location x3 | date |

### Day Parts

```
ca cerni
```
It is morning now. (Now is morning.)

```
ti vanci
```
This is evening.

```
mi sipna ca lo nicte
```
I sleep during the night.

### Time and Date

```
ma temci
```
What time is it?

```
ma detri
```
What is the date?

### Combining with Actions

```
mi citka ca lo cerni
```
I eat during the morning. (I eat breakfast.)

```
mi gunka ca lo vanci
```
I work during the evening.

### Key Point

> `cerni`, `vanci`, `nicte` name parts of the day. `temci` and `detri` refer to time and dates.

## Practice (4-5 min)

### Exercise 1: Day Part Sentences

Translate:

### Exercise 2: Activities by Day Part

Translate:

1. I eat in the morning.
2. I work in the evening.
3. I sleep at night.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. `mi citka ca lo cerni`
2. `mi gunka ca lo vanci`
3. `mi sipna ca lo nicte`

</details>

### Exercise 3: Time Questions

Translate:

1. What time is it?
2. What is the date?
3. The time is short.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. `ma temci`
2. `ma detri`
3. `lo temci cu tordu`

</details>
