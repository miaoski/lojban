## Grammar to Refresh

**EVERYTHING:**

- **Phonology & morphology** — Sound system, word formation rules
- **Basic bridi structure** — x1 selbri x2 x3...
- **cu, .i** — Separators
- **Pronouns** — mi, do, ti, ta, tu, etc.
- **la, doi** — Names and vocatives
- **Numbers** — pa, re, ci... + MEX (su'i, pi'i...)
- **Questions** — xu, ma, mo, xo
- **Place structure** — x1-x5 roles
- **zo'e** — Unspecified/obvious
- **Conversion** — se/te/ve/xe
- **Place tags** — fa/fe/fi/fo/fu
- **Tanru** — Compound predicates
- **bo, ke/ke'e, co** — Tanru grouping
- **Lujvo & fu'ivla** — Compound words, loan words
- **Tense** — pu/ca/ba, vi/va/vu, ki, roi
- **Event contours** — co'a/ca'o/mo'u/co'u
- **Duration** — ze'i/ze'a/ze'u
- **Negation** — na/na'e/to'e/no'e
- **Attitudinals** — .ui, .oi, .au, etc. + intensity (sai/cu'i/nai)
- **Evidentials** — Source of knowledge
- **Abstractions** — nu/du'u/ka/ni
- **Relative clauses** — poi/noi/pe/ne, ke'a
- **Connectives** — Sumti (.e/.a/.o/.u), selbri (je/ja/jo/ju), bridi-tail (gi'e/gi'a), forethought (ge..gi/ga..gi)
- **Sumtcita (BAI)** — ri'a/mu'i/ki'u/bu'u/fa'a/pi'o/ta'i/bau...
- **be/bei/be'o** — Bound arguments
- **Quotation** — lu..li'u, zo, lo'u..le'u
- **la'e/lu'e** — Referent of / symbol for
- **Discursives** — ku'i/ji'a/po'o/mu'a...
- **Pro-bridi** — broda/brode/brodi/brodo/brodu
- **Non-logical connectives** — joi/ce/ce'o
- **Terminator elision** — When to drop ku/kei/ku'o/vau

## Day 1 — Place Structure Marathon

**15 minutes:**
- Select 30 gismu from across all weeks (3 min)
- For each, fill all places with meaningful sumti (7 min)
- Apply se/te/ve/xe conversions to 10 of them (5 min)

**Goal:** Demonstrate command of place structure as the foundation of Lojban.

## Day 2 — Tanru & Lujvo Challenge

**15 minutes:**
- Build 20 creative tanru (8 min)
- Find or create 10 lujvo and decompose them into rafsi (5 min)
- Practice grouping with bo/ke/ke'e and inversion with co (2 min)

**Goal:** Show mastery of compound predicate construction.

## Day 3 — Abstraction & Nesting Drill

**15 minutes:**
- Create 5 sentences with nested nu/du'u abstractions (7 min)
- Practice tu'a shorthand (2 min)
- Use be/bei to bind arguments inside abstractions (3 min)
- Build the most complex sentence you can parse correctly (3 min)

**Goal:** Demonstrate ability to express complex, multi-level thoughts.

## Day 4 — Connectives & Modals Marathon

**15 minutes:**
- Combine sentences using all connective types (7 min):
  - Sumti: .e/.a/.o/.u
  - Selbri: je/ja/jo/ju
  - Bridi-tail: gi'e/gi'a/gi'o/gi'u
  - Forethought: ge..gi/ga..gi
- Practice sumtcita (BAI) for cause, location, instrument, manner (5 min)
- Use non-logical connectives: joi/ce/ce'o (3 min)

**Goal:** Fluently connect ideas in all ways Lojban permits.

## Day 5 — Attitudinals & Tense Narrative

**15 minutes:**
- Write a narrative using varied tenses: pu/ca/ba, vi/va/vu (5 min)
- Add event contours and duration: puca'o, baze'u, etc. (3 min)
- Layer in attitudinals to show emotional journey (4 min)
- Use ki for sticky tense and roi for repetition (3 min)

**Goal:** Tell a vivid story with precise time/space/emotion markers.

## Day 6 — Free Composition (10+ minutes)

**15 minutes:**
- Choose any topic (1 min)
- Write or speak for 10 minutes continuously in Lojban (10 min)
- Use as many grammar structures as naturally fit (4 min)

**Goal:** Demonstrate unrestricted fluency and natural expression.

Topics: your week, a hobby, an explanation, a debate, a story, a plan...

## Day 7 — Self-Assessment & Next Steps

**15 minutes:**
- Review what you've learned over 30 weeks (5 min)
- Identify strongest areas and weakest areas (5 min)
- Plan continued study (5 min):
  - Expand vocabulary (gismu, cmavo, lujvo)
  - Join Lojban community (IRC, Discord, mailing lists)
  - Read Lojban texts (la .lojban., jbovlaste, translations)
  - Write regularly (journal, stories, essays)
  - Speak with other Lojbanists
  - Study CLL (Complete Lojban Language) in depth
  - Explore specialized vocabularies (science, math, art...)
