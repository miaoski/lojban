# Week 12: Rafsi and Lujvo (Compound Word Building)

## Vocabulary to Learn

**New lujvo and meta-vocabulary (8):**
- brivla — x1 is a predicate word expressing relationship x2
- cmavo — x1 is a structure word of grammatical class x2
- lujvo — x1 is a compound word with meaning x2 from components x3
- jbobau — x1 is the Lojban language used by x2 to express x3
- glibau — x1 is the English language used by x2 to express x3
- selsku — x1 is what is said (from se cusku)
- seljmi — x1 is what is understood (from se jimpe)
- terdi — x1 is Earth/the planet (from te dirba)

## Grammar to Learn

**Rafsi and Lujvo Formation:**
- Rafsi: each gismu has 2-5 short combining forms (e.g., klam/kla for klama)
- Lujvo: join rafsi together to make compound words
- Use `y` as buffer if consonant clusters violate Lojban phonotactics
- Example: brivla = briv (from bridi) + la (from valsi) = predicate-word
- Example: jbobau = jbo (from lojbo) + bau (from bangu) = Lojban-language
- jbovlaste dictionary for looking up and decomposing lujvo
- Lujvo have ONE fixed meaning (frozen tanru)
- Tanru are loose/contextual; lujvo are precise/standardized
- Place structure: lujvo inherit complex place structures from their components

## Vocabulary to Refresh

All 98 gismu from weeks 1-11, plus new lujvo:
- Focus on common gismu with short rafsi
- Practice recognizing rafsi in lujvo

## Grammar to Refresh

- Tanru (lujvo are "frozen tanru")
- Grouping with bo and ke...ke'e
- Place structure inheritance

## Conversation Topics

Naming and discussing languages:
- jbobau (Lojban), glibau (English), fasybau (Farsi), etc.
- Decompose lujvo you encounter in texts
- Create simple lujvo for common concepts
- Discuss what is said (selsku) vs what is understood (seljmi)

## Day 1 — Introduction to Rafsi

**15-min practice:**
- Learn brivla, cmavo (5 min)
- Understand rafsi: short combining forms of gismu (5 min)
- Examples: klama → klam, kla; bridi → brid, briv (5 min)
- Each gismu has multiple rafsi for phonotactic flexibility

## Day 2 — Introduction to Lujvo

**15-min practice:**
- Learn lujvo, jbobau (5 min)
- Understand lujvo: compound words from rafsi (5 min)
- Example: jbobau = jbo (lojbo) + bau (bangu) = Lojban language (5 min)
- Practice decomposing: what are the parts?

## Day 3 — The y-hyphen Rule

**15-min practice:**
- Learn glibau, selsku (5 min)
- Understand y-buffer: prevents invalid consonant clusters (5 min)
- Example: if joining creates "ptn", insert y → "pytn" (5 min)
- Practice: when do you need y? When is it optional?

## Day 4 — Common Lujvo Patterns

**15-min practice:**
- Learn seljmi, terdi (5 min)
- Pattern: se + gismu → selsku (what is said), seljmi (what is understood) (5 min)
- Practice: create lujvo with se/te conversions baked in (5 min)
- Example: seltavla (what is talked about, from se tavla)

## Day 5 — Lujvo vs Tanru

**15-min practice:**
- Review all 8 new words (5 min)
- Understand: lujvo = fixed meaning, tanru = contextual meaning (5 min)
- Example: "blanu zdani" (tanru) can mean many things; a lujvo would fix one meaning (5 min)
- When to use which: lujvo for common compounds, tanru for novel descriptions

## Day 6 — Decomposing Lujvo

**15-min practice:**
- Practice breaking down lujvo into component rafsi (5 min)
- Use jbovlaste dictionary to verify (5 min)
- Example: brivla → briv (bridi) + la (valsi) → predicate-word (5 min)
- Drill: given lujvo, identify the gismu components

## Day 7 — Full Week Review

**15-min practice:**
- Flashcard drill: all 8 new lujvo/meta-words (5 min)
- Create simple lujvo for common concepts (5 min)
- Conversation: discuss learning jbobau using brivla, cmavo, lujvo, selsku, seljmi (5 min)
- Example: `mi tadni la .jbobau. .i mi cilre lo brivla .e lo cmavo` — I study Lojban. I learn predicates and particles.
