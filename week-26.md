# Week 26 — Complex Sentence Nesting

## Vocabulary to Learn

1. **jinvi** — x1 has opinion x2 (du'u) about subject x3
2. **tadni** — x1 studies x2 at institution x3
3. **cilre** — x1 learns x2 (du'u/ka) from source x3
4. **ctuca** — x1 teaches x2 (du'u/ka) to x3 by method x4
5. **saske** — x1 is science of/about x2 based on methodology x3
6. **sidbo** — x1 is an idea/concept about x2 in culture/psychology x3
7. **vajni** — x1 is important/significant to x2 (person/situation)
8. **nandu** — x1 is difficult/hard for x2 under conditions x3

## Grammar to Learn

**Nested Abstractions:** Combine multiple abstraction layers.

```
mi djuno lo du'u do djica lo nu klama
```
I know that (du'u) you want to (nu) go.

The outer abstraction (du'u - knowledge) contains an inner abstraction (nu - event).

**tu'a — Raising:** Shortcut for "something involving X."

```
mi djica tu'a la .plise.
```
I want something-involving the apple. (Maybe to eat it, buy it, etc.)

**Conversion Inside Abstractions:**

```
lo nu lo se dunda cu pluka
```
The event of (something) being-given is pleasant. (The receiver's perspective.)

**Parsing Complex Bridi:** Break down multi-level structures systematically:
1. Find the main bridi backbone
2. Identify abstraction boundaries (nu...kei, du'u...kei)
3. Note conversions and their scope
4. Trace relative clauses back to their ke'a

This week integrates ALL grammar learned so far into coherent complex thoughts.

## Vocabulary to Refresh

All approximately 194 gismu learned in weeks 1-25.

## Grammar to Refresh

- Abstractions: nu (event), du'u (proposition), ka (property), ni (amount)
- Conversion: se/te/ve/xe — especially inside nested structures
- Relative clauses: poi/noi with ke'a — can nest inside abstractions
- Place structure: becomes crucial when multiple bridi are nested

## Conversation Topics

Express complex, layered thoughts:
- "I know that Alice wants to learn Lojban."
- "The thing I believe is that science is important."
- "What you taught me is difficult to understand."
- "I think that the idea that you explained is interesting."

Practice nesting at least 2-3 levels deep.

## Day 1 — Simple Nested Abstractions

**15 minutes:**
- Learn jinvi, tadni (3 min)
- Study basic two-level nesting: `mi jinvi lo du'u do tadni la .jbobau.` (5 min)
- Create 5 sentences with opinion/knowledge + inner abstraction (7 min)

## Day 2 — Three Levels Deep

**15 minutes:**
- Learn cilre, ctuca (3 min)
- Practice: `mi djuno lo du'u do djica lo nu cilre` — I know that you want to learn (7 min)
- Build sentences with three abstraction layers (5 min)

## Day 3 — tu'a Shortcut

**15 minutes:**
- Study tu'a raising mechanism (5 min)
- Compare: `mi djica lo nu citka lo plise` vs `mi djica tu'a lo plise` (5 min)
- Practice using tu'a for common wants/likes/needs (5 min)

## Day 4 — Conversion in Nested Contexts

**15 minutes:**
- Learn saske, sidbo (3 min)
- Practice: `lo nu lo se ctuca cu nandu` — The event of being-taught is difficult (7 min)
- Use se/te inside abstractions for perspective shifts (5 min)

## Day 5 — Parsing Practice

**15 minutes:**
- Learn vajni, nandu (3 min)
- Take complex sentences and parse them step-by-step (7 min)
- Example: `mi jinvi lo du'u lo nu tadni la .jbobau. cu vajni lo se cilre` (5 min)

## Day 6 — Relative Clauses in Abstractions

**15 minutes:**
- Combine nesting with relative clauses (7 min)
- `mi djuno lo du'u lo prenu poi ke'a ctuca do cu pendo mi` (5 min)
- Create sentences where relative clauses appear inside abstractions (3 min)

## Day 7 — Full Integration

**15 minutes:**
- Build the most complex sentences yet (10 min)
- Use: nested abstractions + conversion + relative clauses + attitudinals (5 min)
- Example: "I happily know that the person who teaches you thinks that learning Lojban is important."
