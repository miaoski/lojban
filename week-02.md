# Week 02 — `la`, Names & Greetings

## 1. Vocabulary to Learn (8 gismu)

- **cmene** — name
- **prenu** — person
- **nanmu** — man
- **ninmu** — woman
- **verba** — child
- **zdani** — home
- **sutra** — fast
- **barda** — big

## 2. Grammar to Learn

- **`la` article:** `la` marks a name: `la .alis.` (Alice), `la .paris.` (Paris)
- **Lojbanizing names:** Adapt to Lojban phonology, must end in consonant, wrapped in pauses
- **Vocatives:**
  - coi — hello
  - co'o — goodbye
  - ki'e — thanks
  - je'e — acknowledged
  - mi'e — I am [name]
- **Usage:** `coi la .alis.` = Hello, Alice.

## 3. Vocabulary to Refresh

All 12 gismu from Week 1: prami, klama, tavla, citka, pinxe, cadzu, ciska, tcidu, sipna, melbi, xamgu, xlali

## 4. Grammar to Refresh

- Bridi structure (selbri + sumti)
- `cu` marker for selbri
- `.i` sentence separator
- Pronouns: mi, do, ti, ta, tu

## 5. Conversation Topics

- Introduce yourself using `mi'e`
- Greet someone by name
- Say goodbye
- Thank someone

**Example sentence:** `coi la .tam. .i mi'e .alis.` — Hello Tom. I'm Alice.

## Daily Breakdown

### Day 1 — Lojbanizing Your Name

- Pick your name and adapt it to Lojban phonology
- Ensure it ends in a consonant (add s, n, or m if needed)
- Practice saying it with pauses: `.yourname.`
- Say `mi'e .yourname.` 10 times

### Day 2 — Greeting People by Name

- Learn `coi` (hello) and `co'o` (goodbye)
- Practice: `coi la .alis.`, `coi la .bob.`, `coi la .paris.`
- Lojbanize 3 friend/family names
- Greet each one: `coi la .name.`

### Day 3 — Person Words

- Memorize: prenu (person), nanmu (man), ninmu (woman), verba (child)
- Practice: `mi prenu`, `do nanmu`, `la .alis. ninmu`
- Combine with Week 1 verbs: `la .bob. cu klama`, `ti verba cu citka`

### Day 4 — Names in Bridi

- Use `la` in full sentences: `mi tavla la .alis.`
- Practice: `la .bob. cu prami la .alis.`
- Create 5 bridi with named people doing Week 1 actions
- Example: `la .paris. cu melbi` (Paris is beautiful)

### Day 5 — Home and Descriptors

- Learn: zdani (home), sutra (fast), barda (big)
- Practice: `mi klama la zdani` (I go home)
- Describe things: `ti barda`, `do sutra`
- Combine: `la .bob. cu cadzu sutra` (Bob walks fast)

### Day 6 — Full Greetings

- Learn: ki'e (thanks), je'e (acknowledged)
- Practice mini-conversations:
  - `coi` — `coi`
  - `ki'e` — `je'e`
  - `co'o` — `co'o`
- Create 3 greeting sequences using names

### Day 7 — Integration Review

- Introduce yourself to an imaginary person
- Use: `coi`, `mi'e`, a sentence about yourself, `co'o`
- Example: `coi la .alis. .i mi'e .bob. .i mi klama la zdani .i co'o`
- Review all 8 new gismu and all vocatives
