# Week 28 — Reading, Writing & Storytelling

## Vocabulary to Learn

1. **solri** — x1 is the sun of planet/system x2
2. **lunra** — x1 is the moon of planet x2
3. **tarci** — x1 is a star/celestial body
4. **tsani** — x1 is the sky/heavens at place x2
5. **tumla** — x1 is land/ground at location x2
6. **daplu** — x1 is an island in body of water x2
7. **lalxu** — x1 is a lake in location x2
8. **snime** — x1 is snow/ice at location x2

## Grammar to Learn

**PRODUCTION WEEK** — Focus on creating extended Lojban text.

**Storytelling Structures:**

**Sequential Events:**
```
.i ba bo — and then (future + immediately after)
.i pu bo — and previously (past + immediately before)
```

Example: `mi pu cadzu .i ba bo penmi la .tam.` — I walked. And then I met Tom.

**Reported Speech:**

Direct quote: `la .alis. cusku lu mi klama li'u` — Alice says "I go."
Indirect: `la .alis. cusku lo se du'u mi klama` — Alice says that I go.

**Writing Goals This Week:**

1. **Journal entry:** 10+ sentences about your day/week using varied grammar
2. **Short story:** 100+ words with:
   - Clear beginning, middle, end
   - Multiple characters
   - Tense sequencing (past narrative or future speculation)
   - Tanru for vivid description
   - Abstractions for thoughts/feelings
   - Dialogue using reported speech

**Reading Practice:**
- Parse community Lojban texts (jbovlaste examples, la .lojban. wave lessons)
- Identify unfamiliar constructions and look them up
- Practice reading aloud for pronunciation

## Vocabulary to Refresh

All approximately 210 gismu learned in weeks 1-27.

## Grammar to Refresh

- **Tense sequencing:** pu/ca/ba with bo for narrative flow
- **Quotation:** lu..li'u (direct), zo (single word), du'u (indirect)
- **Abstractions in narrative:** characters' thoughts, wants, beliefs
- **Attitudinals:** narrator's and characters' emotions
- **Descriptive tanru:** vivid scene-setting

## Conversation Topics

Tell stories:
- Describe your week: what happened, what you felt, what you learned
- Narrate a memorable experience from your past
- Invent a fictional story with characters and plot
- Describe a dream or imagine a future scenario
- Explain a process or how something works in narrative form

Focus on coherent multi-sentence discourse, not isolated sentences.

## Day 1 — Sequential Narrative

**15 minutes:**
- Learn solri, lunra, tarci (5 min)
- Study `.i ba bo` / `.i pu bo` connectives (3 min)
- Write 5-sentence story using sequential connectives (7 min)

## Day 2 — Reported Speech

**15 minutes:**
- Learn tsani, tumla (3 min)
- Practice direct quotes with lu..li'u (5 min)
- Convert to indirect speech with du'u (4 min)
- Write dialogue between two characters (3 min)

## Day 3 — Descriptive Writing

**15 minutes:**
- Learn daplu, lalxu, snime (3 min)
- Practice descriptive tanru: `lo blanu tsani` (blue sky) (5 min)
- Write vivid scene description using nature vocabulary (7 min)

## Day 4 — Journal Entry

**15 minutes:**
- Write 10+ sentence journal entry about your week (12 min)
- Include: events (tense), feelings (attitudinals), thoughts (abstractions) (3 min)

## Day 5 — Reading Practice

**15 minutes:**
- Find and read a community Lojban text (10 min)
- Parse 3-5 complex sentences, identify grammar structures (5 min)
- Note unfamiliar patterns for further study

## Day 6 — Short Story Draft

**15 minutes:**
- Plan story: characters, setting, plot (3 min)
- Write first half: 50+ words (12 min)

## Day 7 — Short Story Completion

**15 minutes:**
- Complete story: 100+ total words (10 min)
- Review and refine: check grammar, add details (5 min)
- Read aloud for flow and pronunciation
