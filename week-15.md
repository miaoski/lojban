# Week 15 — Tense: Time & Aspect

## 1. Vocabulary to Learn

**10 new gismu:**
- **djedi** — x1 is a day/24-hour period, x2 of week/month x3
- **jeftu** — x1 is a week/7-day period, x2 of month/year x3
- **masti** — x1 is a month/30-day period of year x2
- **nanca** — x1 is a year/365-day period
- **cacra** — x1 is an hour/60-minute period
- **cerni** — x1 is morning/early-day of day x2 at location x3
- **vanci** — x1 is evening of day x2 at location x3
- **nicte** — x1 is night of day x2 at location x3
- **temci** — x1 is a time/moment/duration
- **detri** — x1 is the date/time of event x2 at location x3

## 2. Grammar to Learn

### Tense is Optional in Lojban
- **Tenseless default:** `mi klama` means "I go/went/will go" — context determines when
- Lojban doesn't force you to specify time unless it matters

### Simple Tense: PU/CA/BA
- **pu** — past: `mi pu klama` (I went/I did go)
- **ca** — present: `mi ca klama` (I am going/I go now)
- **ba** — future: `mi ba klama` (I will go)

### Event Contours (Aspect)
- **co'a** — beginning: `mi co'a sipna` (I fall asleep)
- **ca'o** — ongoing/in progress: `mi ca'o gunka` (I am working)
- **mo'u** — completion: `mi mo'u citka` (I finished eating)
- **co'u** — cessation: `mi co'u tavla` (I stop talking)

### Combining Tense + Contour
- **puca'o** — was in the middle of: `mi puca'o citka` (I was eating)
- **baco'a** — will begin: `mi baco'a gunka` (I will start working)
- **pumo'u** — had completed: `mi pumo'u ciska` (I had finished writing)

### Duration
- **ze'i** — short time: `mi ze'i sipna` (I sleep briefly)
- **ze'a** — medium time: `mi ze'a gunka` (I work for a while)
- **ze'u** — long time: `mi ze'u xabju` (I live/dwell for a long time)

## 3. Vocabulary to Refresh

**All prior weeks (~122 words)** — Focus on:
- Action verbs that work well with tense: klama, citka, pinxe, sipna, gunka, tavla
- State verbs to contrast with actions: melbi, xamgu, barda
- Time-related words from earlier weeks

## 4. Grammar to Refresh

- **Bridi structure:** Tenses attach to the bridi, typically right before selbri
- **Place structure:** Understanding x1-x5 helps know what event is being tensed
- **cu:** Separates tense markers from selbri: `mi pu cu klama`

## 5. Conversation Topics

**Topic:** Narrate your day using tenses

**Practice scenarios:**
1. Morning routine: "This morning I ate breakfast"
2. Current activity: "Now I am studying"
3. Evening plans: "Tonight I will sleep"
4. Ongoing actions: "I am in the middle of working"

**Example sentences:**
- `mi pu cerni cu citka` — I ate in the morning (this morning I ate)
- `ca mi ca'o gunka` — Now I am working
- `mi ba vanci cu sipna` — I will sleep in the evening
- `mi puca'o citka` — I was (in the middle of) eating
- `mi ze'u xabju ti` — I live here for a long time

## Day 1 — Time Vocabulary (15 min)

- Learn djedi, jeftu, masti, nanca, cacra
- Practice: `ti djedi` (this is a day)
- Practice: `mi citka ci djedi` (I eat three days... needs fixing)
- Drill: Make sentences about time periods

## Day 2 — Day Parts Vocabulary (15 min)

- Learn cerni, vanci, nicte, temci, detri
- Practice: `ti cerni` (this is morning)
- Practice: `mi ca cerni` (it is morning now / I am morning-like now)
- Better: `ca cerni` (it is morning now)

## Day 3 — Simple Tense (PU/CA/BA) (15 min)

- Learn pu, ca, ba
- Practice: `mi pu klama` (I went)
- Practice: `mi ca citka` (I am eating now)
- Practice: `mi ba sipna` (I will sleep)
- Drill: Translate 10 sentences with simple tense

## Day 4 — Event Contours (15 min)

- Learn co'a, ca'o, mo'u, co'u
- Practice: `mi co'a gunka` (I begin working)
- Practice: `mi ca'o tcidu` (I am reading)
- Practice: `mi mo'u citka` (I finished eating)
- Drill: Describe 5 actions with their contours

## Day 5 — Combining Tense + Contour (15 min)

- Combine pu/ca/ba with co'a/ca'o/mo'u/co'u
- Practice: `mi puca'o tavla` (I was talking)
- Practice: `mi baco'a gunka` (I will start working)
- Practice: `mi pumo'u klama` (I had gone)
- Drill: Create 8 combinations and use in sentences

## Day 6 — Duration (15 min)

- Learn ze'i, ze'a, ze'u
- Practice: `mi ze'i cadzu` (I walk briefly)
- Practice: `mi ze'a gunka` (I work for a while)
- Practice: `mi ze'u xabju ti` (I live here long-term)
- Drill: Add duration to 5 daily activities

## Day 7 — Narrative Practice (15 min)

- Narrate your entire day using all tense tools
- Example: `mi pu cerni cu citka .i ca mi ca'o gunka .i mi ba vanci cu sipna`
  (I ate in the morning. Now I am working. I will sleep in the evening.)
- Practice starting/stopping activities with co'a/co'u
- Self-test: Write a 10-sentence story about yesterday
- Review: When to use tense vs. when to leave it tenseless
