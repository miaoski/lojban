# Week 7: Full Conversion with te, ve, xe

## Vocabulary to Learn

**New gismu (8):**
- banxa — x1 is a bank storing/exchanging x2 for depositor/trader x3
- notci — x1 is a message about x2 from x3 to x4
- fukpi — x1 is a copy/replica of x2 in form/medium x3
- mrilu — x1 is mail/post sent by x2 to x3 with contents x4
- tarja — x1 posts/displays x2 at location x3 for audience x4
- tutci — x1 is a tool for purpose x2 used by x3
- sazri — x1 operates/drives x2 for purpose x3
- zbasu — x1 makes/builds x2 from materials x3

## Grammar to Learn

**te, ve, xe — Full Conversion System:**
- `te` swaps x1↔x3: `te dunda` → x1=recipient, x2=gift, x3=giver
- `ve` swaps x1↔x4: `ve vecnu` → x1=price, x2=goods, x3=buyer, x4=seller
- `xe` swaps x1↔x5: `xe klama` → x1=vehicle, x2=destination, x3=origin, x4=route, x5=goer
- Stacking conversions: `se te` reverses both operations (back to original)
- When to use which: choose conversion based on what you want to emphasize in x1

## Vocabulary to Refresh

Focus on multi-place gismu from weeks 1-6:
- klama (5 places), dunda (3 places), vecnu (4 places), benji (4 places)
- Drill all 56 gismu from previous weeks with emphasis on place structure

## Day 1 — Introduction to te

**15-min practice:**
- Learn banxa, notci (5 min)
- Understand te conversion: swap x1↔x3 (5 min)
- Practice: `te dunda` — x1 receives x2 from x3 (5 min)
- Example: `la .alis. te dunda la .cukta. mi` — Alice receives the book from me

## Day 2 — te Conversion Drills

**15-min practice:**
- Learn fukpi, mrilu (5 min)
- Drill: convert `mi dunda la .cukta. la .alis.` to `la .alis. te dunda la .cukta. mi` (5 min)
- Practice with klama: `te klama` puts origin in x1 (5 min)
- Example: `la .london. te klama la .paris. la .dargu. la .karce. mi` — London is the origin

## Day 3 — Introduction to ve

**15-min practice:**
- Learn tarja, tutci (5 min)
- Understand ve conversion: swap x1↔x4 (5 min)
- Practice: `ve vecnu` — x1=price, x2=goods, x3=buyer, x4=seller (5 min)
- Example: `li mu ve vecnu la .cukta. mi do` — 5 is the price of the book from me to you

## Day 4 — ve Conversion Drills

**15-min practice:**
- Learn sazri, zbasu (5 min)
- Practice ve with benji: `ve benji` — x1=medium of transmission (5 min)
- Drill: when to use ve vs te (5 min)
- Example: `la .mrilu. ve benji la .notci. mi do` — mail is the medium for sending the message

## Day 5 — Introduction to xe

**15-min practice:**
- Review all 8 new gismu (5 min)
- Understand xe conversion: swap x1↔x5 (rare but exists) (5 min)
- Practice: `xe klama` — x1=vehicle (5 min)
- Example: `la .karce. xe klama la .paris. la .london. la .dargu. mi` — the car is the vehicle

## Day 6 — Stacking Conversions

**15-min practice:**
- Practice `se te` — double conversion returns to original order (5 min)
- Try `se ve`, `te ve` combinations (5 min)
- Think through: what does each stack accomplish? (5 min)
- Example: `se te dunda` = `dunda` (x1=giver, x2=gift, x3=recipient)

## Day 7 — Full Week Review

**15-min practice:**
- Flashcard drill: all 8 new gismu (5 min)
- Practice choosing te/ve/xe based on what to emphasize (5 min)
- Conversation: describe a bank transaction using different conversions (5 min)
- Example: `mi vecnu` vs `do se vecnu` vs `la .cukta. te vecnu` vs `li mu ve vecnu`
