# Week 19 — Abstractions: nu, du'u, ka

## 1. Vocabulary to Learn

- **fliba** — x1 fails at doing x2 (state/activity)
- **cfari** — x1 begins/initiates/starts x2 (event/state)
- **fanmo** — x1 is the end/finish of x2
- **sisti** — x1 ceases/stops doing x2
- **dicra** — x1 interrupts/disrupts x2 (event/process)
- **fanta** — x1 prevents/keeps x2 from happening
- **krefu** — x1 recurs/happens again at time x2
- **rapli** — x1 repeats/does again x2 (event)

## 2. Grammar to Learn

**Abstractions** — Turning entire bridi into sumti:

- **nu** — event abstractor: `lo nu mi klama` (the event of me going)
  - Creates a sumti referring to an event/action
  - Most common abstractor for verbs of wanting, beginning, ending

- **du'u** — proposition abstractor: `mi djuno lo du'u do klama` (I know that you go)
  - Creates a sumti referring to a fact/claim/proposition
  - Used with mental predicates: djuno (know), xusra (assert), tugni (agree)

- **ka** — property abstractor: `lo ka melbi` (the property of being beautiful)
  - Creates a sumti referring to a property/quality
  - The abstracted bridi often has `ce'u` as placeholder: `lo ka ce'u melbi` (the property of being beautiful)

- **ni** — amount/degree: `lo ni barda` (the degree/amount of bigness)

- **kei** — abstraction terminator (usually elidable at end of bridi)

Key insight: These turn sentences into things. Just as "eating" is a noun form of "eat," `lo nu citka` makes the event of eating into a grammatical object.

## 3. Vocabulary to Refresh

Focus on mental state verbs that commonly take abstractions:
- **djuno** — know (takes du'u)
- **djica** — want/desire (takes nu)
- **jimpe** — understand (takes du'u)
- **pensi** — think about (takes du'u or nu)
- **pacna** — hope (takes nu)
- **terpa** — fear (takes nu)
- **nelci** — like (takes nu or ka)
- **cfari** — begin (takes nu)
- **fanmo** — end (takes nu)
- **troci** — try (takes nu)
- **snada** — succeed at (takes nu)

## 4. Grammar to Refresh

- **Bridi structure** — Abstractions ARE complete bridi that function as sumti
- **Place structure** — Understanding what goes in each place helps predict which abstraction to use
- **zo'e** — Often used inside abstractions for unspecified places
- **Pronouns** — mi/do/ti work normally inside abstractions

## 5. Conversation Topics

Practice saying:
- "I know that you are going to the store."
- "I want to eat apples."
- "I'm afraid of failing the test."
- "She began to sing."
- "He succeeded at building the house."
- "I understand that Lojban is logical."
- "The property of being beautiful is subjective."

## Day 1 — Introduction to nu

**15 min:** Learn nu (event abstractor) and practice basic sentences.

- Read grammar section on nu
- Memorize: fliba, cfari
- Practice: `mi djica lo nu citka` (I want to eat), `mi cfari lo nu tcidu` (I begin to read)
- Build 3 sentences with nu using verbs you know

## Day 2 — du'u for Facts

**15 min:** Learn du'u (proposition abstractor) for knowledge and belief.

- Read grammar section on du'u
- Memorize: fanmo, sisti
- Practice: `mi djuno lo du'u do klama` (I know that you go)
- Contrast nu vs du'u: events vs facts
- Build 3 sentences with djuno, jimpe, xusra using du'u

## Day 3 — ka for Properties

**15 min:** Learn ka (property abstractor) and ce'u placeholder.

- Read grammar section on ka
- Memorize: dicra, fanta
- Practice: `lo ka melbi` (the property of being beautiful), `mi nelci lo ka cadzu` (I like walking)
- Build 3 sentences with ka describing properties

## Day 4 — Combining Abstractions

**15 min:** Practice using different abstractors in complex sentences.

- Memorize: krefu, rapli
- Practice: `mi djuno lo du'u do djica lo nu klama` (I know that you want to go)
- Build nested abstraction sentences
- Review all 8 vocabulary words

## Day 5 — Mental Verbs + Abstractions

**15 min:** Focus on verbs that take abstractions as arguments.

- Review mental verbs: djuno, djica, pacna, terpa, troci, snada
- Practice each with appropriate abstraction (nu or du'u)
- Examples: `mi terpa lo nu fliba` (I fear failing), `mi pacna lo nu do klama` (I hope you go)

## Day 6 — Conversation Practice

**15 min:** Build conversational exchanges using abstractions.

- Practice: "I know that you want to eat." — `mi djuno lo du'u do djica lo nu citka`
- "I'm afraid of failing." — `mi terpa lo nu fliba`
- "She began to sing." — `ri cfari lo nu sanga`
- Create 5 original conversation exchanges

## Day 7 — Review and Integration

**15 min:** Comprehensive review of week 19 material.

- Quiz yourself on all 8 vocabulary words
- Write 5 complex sentences using nu, du'u, and ka
- Practice saying what you know, want, fear, and like using abstractions
- Review: which abstraction goes with which verb?
