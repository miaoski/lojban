# Week 13 — `co` & Fu'ivla

## 1. Vocabulary to Learn

**8 new gismu:**
- **glare** — x1 is hot/warm with temperature x2 under conditions x3
- **lenku** — x1 is cold/cool with temperature x2 under conditions x3
- **cilmo** — x1 is moist/wet with liquid x2
- **sudga** — x1 is dry of liquid x2
- **jinsa** — x1 is clean/pure of dirt x2
- **curve** — x1 is pure/unmixed, without mixture of x2
- **dirgo** — x1 is a drop/droplet of liquid x2
- **djacu** — x1 is water/H2O

## 2. Grammar to Learn

### `co` — Tanru Inversion
- **`co` inverts tanru order:** `gerku co blanu` = `blanu gerku`
- **Place structure:** comes from the word BEFORE `co` (the "head")
- **Example:** `mi viska lo gerku co blanu` — I see a dog which-is blue (x1 is a dog)
- **Why use it:** Puts the semantically important word first in long tanru chains

### Fu'ivla — Borrowed Words
- **Fu'ivla** are words borrowed from other languages, adapted to Lojban phonology
- **Examples:** `tcati` (tea), `kurki` (cookie), `kafke` (coffee)
- **Morphology rules:**
  - Must contain a consonant cluster (CC)
  - Must end in a vowel
  - Use buffer `y` between consonants if needed
  - Preceded by a pause (`.`) when spoken after another word
- **Creating fu'ivla:** Find source word → adapt to Lojban phonemes → ensure CC → add final vowel

## 3. Vocabulary to Refresh

Focus on **tanru-friendly gismu** from weeks 1-12:
- Color words: blanu, xunre, pelxu, crino, xekri, blabi
- Size/shape: barda, cmalu, clani, tordu, ganra, condi
- Quality: melbi, xamgu, xlali, sutra
- Common nouns: gerku, mlatu, plise, djacu, cukta

## 4. Grammar to Refresh

- **Tanru:** two-word compounds (blanu gerku)
- **bo:** tight tanru binding (sutra bajra bo gerku)
- **ke...ke'e:** grouping tanru (ke sutra bajra ke'e gerku)
- **Rafsi:** short forms for building lujvo
- **Lujvo:** frozen tanru (brivla made from rafsi)

## 5. Conversation Topics

**Topic:** Order drinks and describe foods using `co` and fu'ivla

**Practice scenarios:**
1. Order hot/cold beverages: "I drink hot tea" or "I want cold water"
2. Name foreign foods as fu'ivla
3. Describe food temperatures and qualities
4. Use `co` to put the main noun first

**Example sentences:**
- `mi pinxe la .tcati. co glare` — I drink hot tea
- `mi djica lo djacu co lenku` — I want cold water
- `ti kurki co cilmo` — This is a moist cookie
- `mi citka lo plise co sudga .e lo kurki co jinsa` — I eat dry apples and clean cookies

## Day 1 — Vocabulary Introduction (15 min)

- Learn glare, lenku, cilmo, sudga (temperature and moisture)
- Practice: `lo djacu cu glare` (the water is hot)
- Practice: `lo zdani cu lenku` (the house is cold)
- Drill: Make 5 sentences using temperature words

## Day 2 — More Vocabulary (15 min)

- Learn jinsa, curve, dirgo, djacu
- Practice: `lo djacu cu jinsa` (the water is clean)
- Practice: `mi viska lo dirgo be lo djacu` (I see a water drop)
- Drill: Combine new and old vocab in sentences

## Day 3 — Introduction to `co` (15 min)

- Learn `co` inverts tanru order
- Practice: `gerku co blanu` = `blanu gerku` (but x1 is a dog)
- Practice: `djacu co glare` (hot water, focusing on it being water)
- Drill: Convert 5 regular tanru to `co` form

## Day 4 — `co` Deep Practice (15 min)

- Practice longer tanru with `co`
- Example: `mi pinxe lo djacu co glare co jinsa` (I drink clean hot water)
- Understand when `co` improves readability
- Drill: Create 5 sentences using `co` with new vocab

## Day 5 — Fu'ivla Introduction (15 min)

- Learn fu'ivla rules: consonant cluster, final vowel, pause
- Practice recognizing fu'ivla: tcati, kurki, kafke
- Create 3 fu'ivla from your native language foods
- Practice: `mi pinxe la .tcati.` (I drink tea)

## Day 6 — Fu'ivla Practice (15 min)

- Create 5 more fu'ivla for foods/drinks
- Combine fu'ivla with temperature/quality words
- Practice: `la .kurki. cu cilmo` (the cookie is moist)
- Practice: `mi citka la .kurki. co glare` (I eat hot cookies)

## Day 7 — Review & Integration (15 min)

- Review all 8 new gismu
- Review `co` inversion rules
- Review fu'ivla creation rules
- Practice conversation: Order 5 different drinks/foods using all week's grammar
- Self-test: Translate 10 sentences using this week's material
