# Week 05 — Multi-place Predicate Drills

## 1. Vocabulary to Learn (10 gismu)

- **cukta** — book
- **skami** — computer
- **stizu** — chair
- **jubme** — table
- **kumfa** — room
- **dinju** — building
- **karce** — car
- **jdima** — price
- **zarci** — market
- **rupnu** — currency unit

## 2. Grammar to Learn

- **Two-place drill:** nelci (x1 likes x2), viska (x1 sees x2)
- **Three-place drill:** dunda (x1 gives x2 to x3), tavla (x1 talks to x2 about x3)
- **Four-place drill:** vecnu (x1 sells x2 to x3 for x4)
- **Five-place drill:** klama (x1 goes to x2 from x3 via x4 using x5)
- **Common mistake:** Forcing English word order onto Lojban place order
  - Remember: the gismu definition determines the order, not English grammar

## 3. Vocabulary to Refresh

- **Week 1:** prami, klama, tavla, citka, pinxe, cadzu, ciska, tcidu, sipna, melbi, xamgu, xlali
- **Week 2:** cmene, prenu, nanmu, ninmu, verba, zdani, sutra, barda
- **Week 3:** cmalu, gleki, badri, nelci, djica, gerku, mlatu, plise
- **Week 4:** dunda, vecnu, lebna, bajra, viska, tirna, penmi, djuno, cusku, benji

## 4. Grammar to Refresh

- Place structure concept (x1, x2, x3...)
- `zo'e` for unspecified places
- Left-to-right filling
- `xu` and `go'i` for questions

## 5. Conversation Topics

- "I sell [thing] to [person] for [price]"
- Fill all 5 places of klama
- Talk about objects and locations
- Describe transactions

**Example sentence:** `mi vecnu la .cukta. la .alis. la .rupnu.` — I sell the book to Alice for money.

---

## Daily Breakdown

### Day 1 — Object Vocabulary

- Learn: cukta (book), skami (computer), stizu (chair), jubme (table)
- Practice with two-place verbs: `mi viska la cukta`, `do nelci la skami`
- Practice: `mi citka la plise la .stizu.` (I eat an apple at/on the chair)
- Create 5 sentences describing objects you see

### Day 2 — Location Vocabulary

- Learn: kumfa (room), dinju (building), zarci (market)
- Practice: `mi klama la kumfa`, `ti dinju cu barda`
- Combine with Week 1-2: `la .bob. cu cadzu la zarci`
- Create 5 sentences about locations

### Day 3 — Three-Place Intensive

- Focus on: dunda, tavla, benji, cusku
- Practice tavla with topic: `mi tavla do la cukta` (I talk to you about the book)
- Practice dunda: `mi dunda la skami la .alis.` (I give the computer to Alice)
- Create 8 three-place sentences, at least 2 per gismu

### Day 4 — Four-Place: vecnu with Price

- Learn: jdima (price), rupnu (currency unit), karce (car)
- Practice vecnu with all 4 places: `mi vecnu la karce la .bob. la .rupnu.`
- Practice: `ti cukta cu vecnu zo'e mi la .rupnu.` (This book is sold to me for money)
- Create 5 sentences about buying/selling

### Day 5 — Five-Place: klama Fully Filled

- Review klama: x1 (goer) x2 (destination) x3 (origin) x4 (route) x5 (vehicle)
- Practice with 3 places: `mi klama la zdani la zarci`
- Practice with 4 places: `mi klama la zdani la zarci zo'e`
- Try 5 places: `mi klama la zarci la zdani zo'e la karce` (I go to the market from home via [route] using the car)

### Day 6 — Mix and Match Drill

- Take any 3-place gismu from Week 4
- Take any object from Week 5
- Take any person name
- Build 6 complete sentences
- Example: `la .bob. cu dunda la cukta la .alis.`

### Day 7 — Full Integration Review

- Create a mini-story using 5 sentences
- Use at least one 2-place, one 3-place, and one 4-place gismu
- Include at least 3 objects from this week
- Example: `mi klama la zarci la zdani .i mi viska la cukta .i mi vecnu la cukta la .alis. la .rupnu. .i la .alis. cu gleki`
- Review all place structures from Weeks 4-5
