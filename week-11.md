# Week 11: Tanru Grouping with bo and ke...ke'e

## Vocabulary to Learn

**New gismu (8):**
- skari — x1 is a color of x2
- tarmi — x1 is the shape/form of x2
- bolci — x1 is a ball/sphere of material x2
- kubli — x1 is a cube/regular polyhedron of material x2
- djine — x1 is a ring/torus of material x2
- cinla — x1 is thin in dimension x2 by standard x3
- grake — x1 is a gram of x2 in mass by standard x3
- curve — x1 is pure/unadulterated in property x2 by standard x3

## Grammar to Learn

**Tanru Grouping — bo and ke...ke'e:**
- Default tanru grouping is left-to-right: `a b c` = `(a b) c`
- First two words group, then modify the third
- `bo` binds tighter than default: `a b bo c` = `a (b c)`
- Right side groups first, then left modifies the group
- `ke...ke'e` provides explicit brackets: `a ke b c ke'e` = `a (b c)`
- Resolving ambiguity: `barda blanu gerku` — big blue-dog or big-blue dog?
- `barda blanu bo gerku` — big (blue dog) — intensely blue dog that is big
- `barda bo blanu gerku` — (big blue) dog — dog that is big-blue
- `ke` and `ke'e` for longer groupings with more than 3 words

---

## Day 1 — Default Tanru Grouping

**15-min practice:**
- Learn skari, tarmi (5 min)
- Understand default left-to-right grouping: `a b c` = `(a b) c` (5 min)
- Practice: `barda blanu gerku` defaults to `(barda blanu) gerku` (5 min)
- Example: big-blue dog (a dog that is both big and blue)

## Day 2 — Introduction to bo

**15-min practice:**
- Learn bolci, kubli (5 min)
- Understand bo: binds tighter than default (5 min)
- Practice: `barda blanu bo gerku` = `barda (blanu gerku)` (5 min)
- Example: a big blue-dog (emphasis on blueness)

## Day 3 — bo Practice and Disambiguation

**15-min practice:**
- Learn djine, cinla (5 min)
- Drill: `sutra bajra bo gerku` — fast running-dog vs `sutra bo bajra gerku` — fast-running dog (5 min)
- Practice with colors and sizes (5 min)
- Example: `cmalu xunre bo bolci` — a small red-ball

## Day 4 — Introduction to ke...ke'e

**15-min practice:**
- Learn grake, curve (5 min)
- Understand ke...ke'e as explicit brackets (5 min)
- Practice: `barda ke blanu gerku ke'e` = `barda (blanu gerku)` (5 min)
- Same as `barda blanu bo gerku` but more explicit

## Day 5 — Complex Grouping

**15-min practice:**
- Review all 8 new gismu (5 min)
- Practice longer tanru: 4+ words with ke...ke'e (5 min)
- Example: `ke barda blanu ke'e cmalu gerku` — (big-blue) small dog? (5 min)
- Work through the logic of nested groups

## Day 6 — Ambiguity Resolution Practice

**15-min practice:**
- Given ambiguous tanru, use bo or ke to clarify (5 min)
- Practice: `clani cinla grake` — long thin-rod or long-thin rod? (5 min)
- Drill multiple interpretations and how to specify each (5 min)
- Example: `clani cinla bo grake` vs `clani bo cinla grake`

## Day 7 — Full Week Review

**15-min practice:**
- Flashcard drill: all 8 new gismu (5 min)
- Create complex descriptions with bo and ke...ke'e (5 min)
- Conversation: describe objects with 3+ word tanru, clarify grouping (5 min)
- Example: `ti ke barda blanu ke'e bolci` — this is a big-blue ball (both big and blue)
