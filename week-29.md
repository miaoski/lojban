## Grammar to Learn

**MEX — Mathematical Expressions:**

Use **li** for numbers-as-such (not counting x1/x2).

Operators:
- **su'i** — plus (+)
- **vu'u** — minus (−)
- **pi'i** — times (×)
- **fe'i** — divided by (÷)
- **du** — equals

Example: `li pa su'i re du li ci` — 1 + 2 = 3

**Pro-bridi (Placeholder Predicates):**

- **broda, brode, brodi, brodo, brodu** — stand-in for selbri

Use when you want to refer back to a predicate without repeating it:
```
mi tavla fo la .lojban. .i do broda — I talk in Lojban. You [do-the-same-predicate].
```

**Terminator Elision:**

Many terminators can be safely dropped:
- **ku** (end sumti) — when bridi ends or another structure starts
- **kei** (end abstraction) — at end of bridi
- **ku'o** (end relative clause) — before new bridi
- **vau** (end bridi) — before `.i`

Only required when ambiguity would arise. Most everyday Lojban omits them.

**Non-Logical Connectives:**

- **joi** — mass-and (joint/together): `la .alis. joi la .tam. cu pendo` (Alice-and-Tom [as a unit] are friends)
- **ce** — set-and (list members): `lo plise ce lo perli` (apples and pears)
- **ce'o** — sequence-and (ordered): `mi ce'o do ce'o la .tam. cu klama` (I, then you, then Tom go — in that order)

These are finishing touches for nuanced expression.

**15 minutes:**
- Learn turni, kagni (3 min)
- Study MEX operators: su'i, vu'u, pi'i, fe'i (5 min)
- Practice: `li re pi'i ci su'i pa du li ze` — 2 × 3 + 1 = 7 (7 min)

## Day 2 — More Math Practice

**15 minutes:**
- Learn jibri (2 min)
- Create 10 math equations in Lojban (8 min)
- Practice order of operations: `li pa su'i re pi'i ci du li ze` — 1 + (2 × 3) = 7 (5 min)
