# Week 10: Two-Word Tanru

## Vocabulary to Learn

**New gismu (10):**
- blanu — x1 is blue
- xunre — x1 is red
- pelxu — x1 is yellow
- crino — x1 is green
- xekri — x1 is black
- blabi — x1 is white
- clani — x1 is long in dimension x2 by standard x3
- tordu — x1 is short in dimension x2 by standard x3
- ganra — x1 is wide/broad in dimension x2 by standard x3
- condi — x1 is deep/has depth in range x2 by standard x3

## Grammar to Learn

**Tanru — Two-Word Modification:**
- Tanru: two gismu side by side — left modifies right
- Place structure comes from the RIGHTMOST word
- `sutra klama` has the places of klama (goer, destination, origin, route, vehicle)
- Tanru meaning is vague/loose — context determines exact interpretation
- `gerku zdani` could mean: house for dogs, dog-like house, house shaped like dog, etc.
- Creating adjective-like descriptions: `blanu gerku` (blue dog), `barda zdani` (big house)
- Modifying selbri with properties: `sutra bajra` (fast running), `xamgu citka` (good eating)

## Vocabulary to Refresh

All 80 gismu from weeks 1-9, with focus on:
- Size/quality words: barda, cmalu, xamgu, xlali, sutra, melbi
- Objects: gerku, mlatu, zdani, plise, cukta
- Gismu that combine well in tanru

## Grammar to Refresh

- Place structure (tanru take rightmost word's structure)
- Conversion (still applies to tanru)
- Multi-word selbri basics

## Conversation Topics

Describe things with colors, sizes, and qualities:
- "The big blue dog"
- "The small red cat"
- "A fast-running person"
- "A good-eating apple"
- Ambiguity in tanru interpretation

## Day 1 — Introduction to Tanru

**15-min practice:**
- Learn blanu, xunre, pelxu (5 min)
- Understand tanru: left modifies right (5 min)
- Practice: `blanu gerku` — blue dog (5 min)
- Example: `ti blanu gerku` — this is a blue dog

## Day 2 — Tanru Place Structure

**15-min practice:**
- Learn crino, xekri, blabi (5 min)
- Understand: place structure comes from RIGHTMOST word (5 min)
- Practice: `sutra klama` has klama's 5 places (5 min)
- Example: `mi sutra klama la .paris.` — I fast-go to Paris (I go to Paris quickly)

## Day 3 — Color Tanru Practice

**15-min practice:**
- Learn clani, tordu (5 min)
- Practice color descriptions: blanu/xunre/crino/pelxu with objects (5 min)
- Drill: `xunre mlatu`, `pelxu plise`, `crino zdani` (5 min)
- Example: `ti xunre mlatu` — this is a red cat

## Day 4 — Size and Dimension Tanru

**15-min practice:**
- Learn ganra, condi (5 min)
- Practice with barda, cmalu, clani, tordu, ganra (5 min)
- Example: `barda zdani` — big house, `clani karce` — long car (5 min)
- Drill: combine size words with objects from previous weeks

## Day 5 — Quality and Property Tanru

**15-min practice:**
- Review all 10 new gismu (5 min)
- Practice xamgu, xlali, melbi, sutra with actions (5 min)
- Example: `sutra bajra` — fast running, `xamgu citka` — good eating (5 min)
- Note: `xamgu citka` could mean "eating well" or "good food"

## Day 6 — Tanru Ambiguity

**15-min practice:**
- Explore loose tanru meanings (5 min)
- `gerku zdani` — dog house? dog-like house? house shaped like dog? (5 min)
- Context determines interpretation (5 min)
- Example: `ti gerku zdani .i la .spot. zdani` — this is a dog house (Spot's house)

## Day 7 — Full Week Review

**15-min practice:**
- Flashcard drill: all 10 new gismu (5 min)
- Create descriptions using color + size + object (5 min)
- Conversation: describe your room using tanru (5 min)
- Example: `mi zo'e barda blanu stizu .i la .stizu. cu xamgu` — I [sit on] a big blue chair. The chair is good.
