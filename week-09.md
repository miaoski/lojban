# Week 9: Place Structure Review

## Vocabulary to Learn

**New gismu (8):**
- bilga — x1 is obliged/required to do x2 by x3
- curmi — x1 allows/permits x2 to do x3 under conditions x4
- sarcu — x1 is necessary/requisite for x2 under conditions x3
- pacna — x1 hopes/wishes for x2 to be true
- terpa — x1 fears x2
- tatpi — x1 is tired/fatigued from x2
- xagji — x1 is hungry for x2
- taske — x1 is thirsty for x2

## Grammar to Learn

**Comprehensive Place Structure Review:**
- Review all conversion methods: se (x1↔x2), te (x1↔x3), ve (x1↔x4), xe (x1↔x5)
- Review all place tags: fa, fe, fi, fo, fu
- Drill: given English, choose correct place filling method
- Drill: rewrite bridi with se/te/ve using fa/fe/fi tags instead
- Drill: apply conversions and explain the new meaning
- Common pitfalls: forgetting which place is which, mixing up te/ve
- Best practices: when to use conversion vs tags

## Vocabulary to Refresh

**Randomized flashcard drill of all 72 gismu from weeks 1-8:**
- Focus on place structure of each gismu
- Practice saying the x1, x2, x3... definitions
- Identify which gismu have 4+ places

## Grammar to Refresh

**Everything from Phase 2:**
- Place structure basics
- zo'e for unspecified places
- se/te/ve/xe conversion system
- fa/fe/fi/fo/fu place tags
- Multi-place predicates

## Conversation Topics

Complex scenarios using full place structures:

## Day 1 — Review se Conversion

**15-min practice:**
- Learn bilga, curmi (5 min)
- Drill 10 bridi with se conversion (5 min)
- Practice: `mi dunda` vs `do se dunda` vs `la .cukta. te dunda` (5 min)
- Ensure clarity on x1↔x2 swap

## Day 2 — Review te and ve Conversion

**15-min practice:**
- Learn sarcu, pacna (5 min)
- Drill te (x1↔x3) with dunda, klama, benji (5 min)
- Drill ve (x1↔x4) with vecnu, benji (5 min)
- Common mistake: confusing te and ve

## Day 3 — Review Place Tags

**15-min practice:**
- Learn terpa, tatpi (5 min)
- Drill fa/fe/fi/fo/fu with complex sentences (5 min)
- Practice: `fi la .paris. fo la .dargu. fu la .karce. fa mi cu klama fe la .london.` (5 min)
- Out-of-order place filling

## Day 4 — Conversion to Tags Translation

**15-min practice:**
- Learn xagji, taske (5 min)
- Take 5 bridi with conversions, rewrite using tags (5 min)
- Take 5 bridi with tags, rewrite using conversions (5 min)
- Example: `te dunda` → `fi` in x1 with fa/fe for others

## Day 5 — Complex Multi-Place Drills

**15-min practice:**
- Review all 8 new gismu (5 min)
- Practice vecnu with all 4 places: seller, goods, buyer, price (5 min)
- Practice klama with all 5 places: goer, destination, origin, route, vehicle (5 min)
- Example: `mi vecnu la .cukta. do li mu` — I sell the book to you for 5

## Day 6 — Error Correction Practice

**15-min practice:**
- Common errors: wrong conversion, wrong tag, wrong place order (5 min)
- Practice: identify and fix broken bridi (5 min)
- Drill: explain why each fix is correct (5 min)
- Example: `fi do fa mi dunda` is wrong — should be `fi do fa mi cu dunda fe la .cukta.`

## Day 7 — Full Week Review

**15-min practice:**
- Flashcard drill: all 8 new gismu (5 min)
- Complex conversation: describe a shopping trip with full place structures (5 min)
- Challenge: use klama, vecnu, benji, notci in one story with all places filled (5 min)
- Example: `mi klama la .zarci. la .zdani. la .dargu. la .karce. .i mi vecnu la .plise. la .prenu. li mu`
