# Week 25 — Advanced Tense & Space

## Vocabulary to Learn

1. **nenri** — x1 is inside/within x2
2. **bartu** — x1 is outside x2
3. **gapru** — x1 is above/over x2
4. **cnita** — x1 is below/under x2
5. **crane** — x1 is in front of x2
6. **trixe** — x1 is behind x2
7. **zunle** — x1 is to the left of x2
8. **pritu** — x1 is to the right of x2

## Grammar to Learn

**Spatial Tenses:** In Lojban, space is treated as a tense just like time.

- **vi** — here, near (spatially close)
- **va** — there, mid-distance
- **vu** — yonder, far away

**Compound Tenses:** Combine time and event contours for precise meaning.

- **puca'o** — was ongoing (past + continuous)
- **baco'a** — will begin (future + initiative)
- **puze'a** — for a while in the past (past + medium duration)

**ki — Sticky Tense:** Set a tense that persists across multiple sentences.

```
mi pu ki klama la .paris. .i klama la .london.
```
(In the past:) I went to Paris. I went to London. (Both actions share the `pu` tense.)

**roi — Repetition Counter:**

- **paroi** — once
- **re roi** — twice
- **so'i roi** — many times

Space and time are symmetrical: just as `pu` means "before now," spatial tenses describe distance from "here."

## Vocabulary to Refresh

All approximately 186 gismu learned in weeks 1-24.

## Grammar to Refresh

- Basic temporal tenses: pu (past), ca (present), ba (future)
- Event contours: co'a (beginning), ca'o (ongoing), mo'u (completed), co'u (ceased)
- Duration: ze'i (brief), ze'a (medium), ze'u (long)
- Sumtcita (BAI tags) — spatial tenses extend these concepts

## Conversation Topics

Describe journeys and locations using spatial and temporal tenses:
- "Yesterday I was at the mountain. I walked far. Twice I rested."
- "The book is on the table. The table is in the room."
- "I will go to the city tomorrow. I have been there many times."

Practice combining space and time: "I was far away for a long time."

## Day 1 — Spatial Tenses Basics

**15 minutes:**
- Learn nenri, bartu (5 min)
- Study vi/va/vu spatial tenses (5 min)
- Practice: "The cat is inside the house" — `lo mlatu cu nenri vi lo zdani` (5 min)

## Day 2 — Above, Below, Front, Back

**15 minutes:**
- Learn gapru, cnita, crane, trixe (7 min)
- Practice combining with spatial distance: `lo mlatu cu gapru va lo jubme` — The cat is above the table (mid-distance) (5 min)
- Create 5 sentences using these gismu (3 min)

## Day 3 — Left and Right

**15 minutes:**
- Learn zunle, pritu (3 min)
- Practice full spatial descriptions: position + distance (7 min)
- Describe your room using all 8 spatial gismu (5 min)

## Day 4 — Compound Tenses

**15 minutes:**
- Study puca'o, baco'a, puze'a (5 min)
- Practice: `mi puca'o citka` — I was eating (5 min)
- Create sentences combining time, event, and duration (5 min)

## Day 5 — Sticky Tense with ki

**15 minutes:**
- Learn ki mechanism (5 min)
- Practice: Set `pu ki` and tell a multi-sentence story in past tense (7 min)
- Compare with/without ki to understand efficiency (3 min)

## Day 6 — Repetition with roi

**15 minutes:**
- Learn roi and number combinations (5 min)
- Practice: `mi re roi klama la .paris.` — I went to Paris twice (3 min)
- Describe habits and repeated actions: `mi ro djedi cadzu` + `so'i roi` (7 min)

## Day 7 — Integration Practice

**15 minutes:**
- Describe a complete journey using all week's tools (10 min)
- Example: "Yesterday I walked to the mountain far away. Three times I rested. I was walking for a long time. The view above was beautiful." (5 min)
- Review all 8 vocabulary items in context
