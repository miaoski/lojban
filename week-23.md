# Week 23 — be/bei/be'o — Places Inside Descriptions

## 1. Vocabulary to Learn

- **lerfu** — x1 is a letter/character in alphabet x2 representing x3
- **papri** — x1 is a page/leaf of book/document x2
- **jufra** — x1 is a sentence/statement about x2 in language x3
- **pinka** — x1 is a comment/remark about x2 by x3
- **sitna** — x1 cites/quotes/refers to x2 for audience x3
- **bacru** — x1 utters/says x2 (phonemes/words)
- **karni** — x1 is a journal/periodical with content x2
- **datni** — x1 is data/information about x2

## 2. Grammar to Learn

**be/bei/be'o** — Filling places inside descriptions and tanru:

**The Problem:**
When you use a selbri as a description or in a tanru, how do you specify its other places?
- `la vecnu` — "the seller" but seller of WHAT? to WHOM?

**The Solution:**

- **be** — attaches x2 to a selbri
  - `la vecnu be la .skami.` (the seller of-computers)
  - Binds the x2 place inside the description

- **bei** — attaches x3, x4, x5 (further places)
  - `la vecnu be la .skami. bei mi` (the seller of-computers to-me)
  - Can chain: `be x2 bei x3 bei x4`

- **be'o** — terminates the be/bei structure
  - Needed when more structure follows
  - `la vecnu be la .skami. be'o poi barda` (the seller of-computers, who is big)
  - Often elidable at end of description

**Common Uses:**

1. **In descriptions:**
   - `la tavla be la .jbobau.` (the speaker of-Lojban)
   - `lo dunda be lo plise bei mi` (the giver of-apples to-me)

2. **In tanru:**
   - `la skami be la .tivni. nolraitru` (the TV-computer leader)
   - Specifies which kind of computer

3. **With relative clauses:**
   - `la vecnu be lo cukta be'o poi melbi` (the book-seller who is beautiful)
   - be'o separates the be-structure from the poi-clause

**Key insight:**
Without be/bei, places inside descriptions are ambiguous. `la cukta vecnu` could mean "book seller" (tanru) but doesn't say which books. `la vecnu be lo cukta` specifically means "seller of-books."

## 3. Vocabulary to Refresh

Focus on multi-place selbri commonly used in descriptions:
- **vecnu** — x1 sells x2 to x3 for price x4
- **dunda** — x1 gives x2 to x3
- **tavla** — x1 talks to x2 about x3 in language x4
- **ciska** — x1 writes x2 on x3 with tool x4
- **tcidu** — x1 reads x2 from source x3
- **ctuca** — x1 teaches x2 (facts) to x3 about subject x4
- **lebna** — x1 takes x2 from x3
- **benji** — x1 sends x2 to x3 from x4

## Day 1 — Introduction to be

**15 min:** Learn to attach x2 with be.

- Read grammar section on be
- Memorize: lerfu, papri
- Practice: `la tavla be la .jbobau.` (the speaker of-Lojban)
- Practice: `la vecnu be lo plise` (the seller of-apples)
- Build 3 descriptions using be to specify x2

## Day 2 — Extending with bei

**15 min:** Learn to attach x3, x4, x5 with bei.

- Read grammar section on bei
- Memorize: jufra, pinka
- Practice: `la dunda be lo plise bei mi` (the giver of-apples to-me)
- Practice: `la tavla be la .lojban. bei mi` (the talker about-Lojban to-me)
- Build 3 descriptions using be...bei

## Day 3 — The be'o Terminator

**15 min:** Learn when be'o is needed.

- Read grammar section on be'o
- Memorize: sitna, bacru
- Practice: `la vecnu be lo cukta be'o poi barda` (the book-seller who is big)
- Without be'o: ambiguous! Does poi modify cukta or vecnu?
- Build 3 sentences where be'o is essential

## Day 4 — be/bei in Tanru

**15 min:** Use be/bei to specify tanru components.

- Read grammar on be/bei in tanru
- Memorize: karni, datni
- Practice: `la skami be la .tivni. pilno` (the computer-of-TV user)
- Clarifies which element of the tanru takes the place
- Build 3 tanru with be/bei

## Day 5 — Complex Descriptions

**15 min:** Combine be/bei with other grammar.

- Review all 8 vocabulary words
- Practice: `la ctuca be la .cmaci. bei lo verba` (the teacher of-math to-children)
- Combine with abstractions: `la djica be lo nu klama` (the wanter of-going)
- Build 3 complex descriptions

## Day 6 — Conversation Practice

**15 min:** Describe people by their relationships and actions.

- Practice: "the speaker of Lojban" — `la tavla be la .jbobau.`
- "the seller of books to me" — `la vecnu be lo cukta bei mi`

- Create 5 conversational exchanges describing people

## Day 7 — Review and Integration

**15 min:** Comprehensive review of week 23 material.

- Quiz yourself on all 8 vocabulary words
- Write 5 descriptions using be, bei, and be'o
- Practice: When is be'o elidable? When is it required?
- Review how be/bei interact with tanru and relative clauses
