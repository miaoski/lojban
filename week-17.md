# Week 17 — Questions Deep Dive

## 1. Vocabulary to Learn

**8 new gismu:**
- **mukti** — x1 is a motive/reason for action x2 by agent x3
- **rinka** — x1 (event) causes x2 (event) under conditions x3
- **jalge** — x1 is a result/outcome of x2 (event/cause)
- **krinu** — x1 is a reason/justification for x2 (event/state)
- **gasnu** — x1 (agent) causes/brings about x2 (event)
- **zukte** — x1 acts/does x2 (action)
- **troci** — x1 tries to do x2 (action)
- **snada** — x1 succeeds in doing x2 (action)

## 2. Grammar to Learn

### `ma` — Fill-in-the-Blank for Sumti
- **Question word for arguments (sumti):** Replaces x1, x2, etc.
- **Examples:**
  - `do klama ma` — Where are you going? (you go to [what]?)
  - `ma klama` — Who/what goes?
  - `do dunda ma mi` — What do you give me?
  - `do dunda ti ma` — To whom do you give this?

### `mo` — Fill-in-the-Blank for Selbri
- **Question word for predicates (selbri):** What is/does X?
- **Examples:**
  - `do mo` — What are you (doing)? / What's your state?
  - `ti mo` — What is this?
  - `do klama mo` — You go how? (manner question, unusual usage)

### `xo` — Fill-in-the-Blank for Numbers
- **Question word for quantities:**
- **Examples:**
  - `xo gerku` — How many dogs?
  - `do ponse xo cukta` — You possess how-many books?
  - `ti jdima xo` — This costs how-much?

### Multiple Question Words
- **You can use multiple `ma` in one bridi:**
  - `ma dunda ma ma` — Who gives what to whom?
  - Context usually makes the answer clear
- **Answer by filling the blanks in order:**
  - Question: `ma klama ma` (Who goes where?)
  - Answer: `la .tam. klama lo zarci` (Tom goes to the market)

### Answering Questions
- **For `ma`:** Replace `ma` with the answer
  - Q: `do klama ma` — A: `mi klama lo zarci` (I go to the market)
- **For `mo`:** Replace `mo` with the selbri
  - Q: `do mo` — A: `mi gunka` (I work) or `mi gunka` (I'm working)
- **For `xo`:** Replace `xo` with the number
  - Q: `do ponse xo cukta` — A: `mi ponse mu cukta` (I possess 5 books)

## 3. Vocabulary to Refresh

**All prior weeks (~138 words)** — Focus on:
- **xu** (yes/no question marker): `xu do klama` (Do you go?)
- **go'i** (repeat previous bridi): Used for "yes" answers
- **na go'i** (negated repeat): Used for "no" answers
- Common verbs to ask about: klama, citka, djica, zukte

## 4. Grammar to Refresh

- **xu** for yes/no questions: `xu do nelci ti` (Do you like this?)
- **go'i** for confirming: `go'i` (yes, repeat the claim)
- **Negation with questions:** `xu do na klama` (Is it true you don't go?)
- **na go'i** for denial: `na go'i` (no, negate the claim)

## 5. Conversation Topics

**Topic:** Interview someone about their actions and motivations

**Practice scenarios:**
1. Ask what someone is doing: `do mo`
2. Ask where they're going: `do klama ma`
3. Ask why: `do zukte ti mu'i ma` (You do this motivated-by what?)
4. Ask how many: `do ponse xo gerku`

**Example sentences:**
- `do mo` — What are you (doing)?
- `do klama ma` — Where are you going?
- `do zukte ma` — What are you doing? (what action?)
- `ma rinka lo nu do gleki` — What causes you to be happy?
- `do troci lo nu gasnu ma` — You try to cause what?
- `do snada mu'i ma` — You succeed motivated-by what?
- `xo prenu cu klama` — How many people go?

## Day 1 — Cause/Effect Vocabulary (15 min)

- Learn mukti, rinka, jalge, krinu
- Practice: `ti rinka lo nu mi gleki` (This causes me to be happy)
- Practice: `ti jalge lo nu mi gunka` (This is a result of me working)
- Practice: `ma mukti lo nu do klama` (What motivates you to go?)
- Drill: Make 5 cause-effect sentences

## Day 2 — Action Vocabulary (15 min)

- Learn gasnu, zukte, troci, snada
- Practice: `mi zukte ti` (I do this)
- Practice: `mi troci lo nu gunka` (I try to work)
- Practice: `mi snada lo nu klama` (I succeed in going)
- Drill: Describe 5 actions with try/succeed

## Day 3 — `ma` for Sumti Questions (15 min)

- Learn `ma` replaces any sumti (argument)
- Practice: `do klama ma` (Where do you go?)
- Practice: `ma klama lo zarci` (Who goes to the market?)
- Practice: `do dunda ma mi` (What do you give me?)
- Drill: Create 10 `ma` questions and answer them

## Day 4 — `mo` for Selbri Questions (15 min)

- Learn `mo` replaces the selbri (predicate)
- Practice: `do mo` (What are you / what are you doing?)
- Practice: `ti mo` (What is this?)
- Compare with `ma`: `do mo` vs. `do zukte ma`
- Drill: Ask "what is X?" for 10 objects

## Day 5 — `xo` for Number Questions (15 min)

- Learn `xo` asks for quantities
- Practice: `xo gerku cu cadzu` (How many dogs walk?)
- Practice: `do ponse xo cukta` (You own how many books?)
- Practice: `ti jdima xo` (This costs how much?)
- Drill: Ask quantity questions about 10 scenarios

## Day 6 — Multiple Questions & Complex Queries (15 min)

- Practice multiple `ma` in one bridi
- Practice: `ma dunda ma mi` (Who gives what to me?)
- Combine question words: `xo prenu cu klama ma` (How many people go where?)
- Practice with cause words: `ma rinka ma` (What causes what?)
- Drill: Create 5 complex multi-question sentences

## Day 7 — Interview Practice (15 min)

- Conduct a full interview using all question types
- Ask: What are you doing? (`do mo`)
- Ask: Where are you going? (`do klama ma`)
- Ask: Why? (`do zukte ti mu'i ma` or `ma mukti`)
- Ask: How many? (`do ponse xo X`)
- Self-test: Create 10-question interview and answer all
- Review: Difference between xu, ma, mo, xo
- Final practice: Role-play interviewer and interviewee
